<summary><strong>Details: requirements, commands, teams and quest files</strong></summary>

<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Fabric: Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer), 26.2 or 26.1.2 (0.18.4 or newer), 1.21.11 (0.17 or newer), 1.21.1 (0.16 or newer) or 1.20.1 (0.15 or newer), each with Fabric API.</li>
<li>NeoForge: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1 or newer. Forge: Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.1 or newer. No other mod needed; JEI and other recipe viewers list all icons (search <strong>@questsicons</strong>). On Forge 1.20.1, JEI must also be on the server.</li>
<li>On a server, install it on the server and on every client.</li>
<li>On 1.20.1, items in quest files use the 1.20.1 NBT. Importing an FTB Quests book of 1.21 or 26.1 there leaves out item data that 1.20.1 cannot read; the report lists it.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Commands</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Everyone: <strong>/quests open</strong> and <strong>/quests team info | create | invite | join | leave</strong>. Operators: <strong>reload</strong>, <strong>givebook</strong>, <strong>complete</strong>, <strong>reset</strong> and <strong>import ftbquests [preview | run | merge] [folder]</strong>. <strong>/questbook</strong> works the same.</li>
<li>Joining a team replaces your progress with the team's. Leaving starts you over with empty progress.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Quest files</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Commas are optional. IDs are 16-digit hex numbers or any readable name.</li>
<li>Text takes &amp; colour codes, translation keys like {mypack.quest.title} and JSON text. Pictures: {image:namespace:path.png width:120 height:60}.</li>
<li>Rewards can go to the whole team (<strong>team_reward</strong>) or be claimed on their own (<strong>auto</strong>).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>In-game editor (operators)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Map: right-click for <strong>New quest here</strong>, <strong>Paste</strong>, <strong>Add picture here</strong> and the chapter settings; right-click a quest for Edit, Choose icon, Copy, Duplicate, Move to chapter, links and Delete (click twice).</li>
<li>Drag quests (Snap on/off in the top bar), Shift-drag from one quest to the next to link them, click a line to remove it, Ctrl-click or Ctrl-drag to select several. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Delete and Ctrl+Z (Undo) work in edit mode.</li>
<li>Chapter list: <strong>+ Chapter</strong>, <strong>+ Group</strong>, drag chapters to reorder them, right-click a chapter or group for its form. The top bar has <strong>Book</strong> (settings) and <strong>Tables</strong> (reward tables and loot crates).</li>
<li>Click a task or reward icon in the quest window to choose its icon. Every change is saved to the SNBT files and sent to every player at once.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>FTB Quests import</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>preview</strong> (the default) changes nothing and writes <strong>config/questsicons/ftbquests-import-report.txt</strong>. <strong>run</strong> replaces the book, <strong>merge</strong> keeps your own chapters and adds the FTB ones. Both back up your quest files to <strong>config/questsicons/backups/</strong> first.</li>
<li>The folder is <strong>config/ftbquests/quests</strong> by default; an instance folder, a config folder or a full path works too.</li>
<li>Fluid, energy, game stage and KubeJS tasks become checkmark tasks with a clear title. Currency, game stage and KubeJS rewards are skipped. The report lists every case and every id of a mod that is not installed.</li>
</ul>
