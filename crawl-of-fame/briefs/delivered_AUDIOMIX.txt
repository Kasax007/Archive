Summary of the lead (the changes are on the branch; see docs/reports/VOICE.md, "Decisions of the lead after the review", and docs/VOICE.md, "Hearing the game"): the test client can record its own sound (CT_AUDIO=1 in tools/crawltest/run.sh: OpenAL's wave writer; script commands pace, vol, beep, amark in CtScript; script tools/crawltest/scripts/listen.txt) and tools/crawltest/listen.py listens to the recording (a recogniser hears every line the voice director started; levels between the script's marks). What the recordings showed led to: music at level 0.5 (MusicDirector.LEVEL, was 0.8) and 11 dB down under a voice (VoiceDirector.MUSIC_DUCK); a voice waits for the loud part of a cue (VoiceQueue.hold, ClientShow.leadMs, VoiceDirector.cue) and the cue steps back 9 dB under the voice (audio.CueSound, Duck.cue); the boss card plays through ClientShow.play and speaks its introduction itself (BossHud.card); placeholder recordings never play for a player (VoiceIndex.withoutDrafts unless -Dcrawl.voice.drafts=true, which the crawltest run sets except with CT_AUDIO); while only some variants of an occasion are recorded the Emcee says those (Emcee.pick, Emcee.voiced set by VoiceModule.recordedKeys from the index in the jar); one crate jingle per player and tick, the best crate's (audio.CrateJingles); the achievements unlocked in one tick are one chat line (Achievements.tellUnlocks, titles); the levels gained in one tick get one line (ShowFlow.levelLines); Prop Storage without a zone boss vault (the smallest depth): the last freight switch calls the freight lift, a crate for every crawler on the floor (Floor2Rule, hint freight_lift, line rules.objective.freight.delivered); process.py keeps a line's loudness when the encoder overshoots. Tests the lead ran: jc, the full game tests, the listening script with listen.py (all checks passed; six of six lines heard as written in the third recording). New game tests: voice_game_tests_a_cue_holds_the_voice_back, voice_game_tests_emcee_prefers_recorded_variants, rules_game_tests_freight_without_a_vault_delivers_a_crate, audio_game_tests_crate_jingles_one_at_a_time, show_game_tests_unlocks_of_one_tick_are_one_line. Not covered by a test: the client side (CueSound, the hold set by a cue, BossHud) except through the listening run; nobody has heard the result with human ears yet (the owner will).
