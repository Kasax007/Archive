TASK __ID__: the item in action. The owner of the mod wrote "More animations on items too" and puts shapes, textures and item animation fourth on a list of eight wishes. The task MODELS gave every held item a model, the armour sets a shape and drops a beam, and its review round fixed how things are HELD (shields are raised into the picture, two hands on a two-handed weapon, the game's poses for bow and crossbow, beams that read in daylight): read docs/reports/MODELS.md to its end (the reviews' findings and the lead's decisions are there), docs/ITEMS-ART.md, and look at every sheet in /home/user/shots/MODELS/ and /home/user/shots/MODELS/review/. What MODELS left out is everything that happens while an item is USED, and that is where a player judges gear: in the half second of a swing. Today every weapon of the mod swings the game's one swing, a full blow looks like a tap, a blocked hit does nothing to the shield, a cast is a stick held still. You make the gear move. Reports to read besides MODELS: B3-items (bases, weapon classes, reach, attack speed), B2-profile (Combat: what a full blow and a blocked hit are on the server), A6-magic (casting, schools, the spell bar), UI1 (reduced motion, the client config), AUDIO-sound (the sounds a swing, a block and a cast have today).

THE BAR: a player who picks up a maul after a dagger must feel the difference without reading a number. Nothing snaps: every motion has a wind-up, a strike and a recovery with eased curves, and returns to rest without a jump. First person and third person tell the same story, and another player sees it too. Combat itself does not change: the hit lands when and where it lands today, the numbers are the server's (this task is client side; a small payload where the client cannot know something is fine and must be validated like any other).

DELIVER
1. A small clip system for held items (client): named clips (keyframes for the item and the arms, eased, in seconds), chosen by weapon class and situation, played in first person (the hand and item transform) and third person (arms, a little body turn), for the local player and for other players and mannequins from what the client already receives (the swing packet, the use state, the held item). The task BUILDS1 will add a technique per weapon class later: it must be able to add a clip by writing one description, not by another mixin. Injection points that other mods also touch (the first-person item renderer, the humanoid model's arm setup): the smallest possible hooks, no overwrite, no redirect; say in the report which ones.
2. A swing per weapon class, light and full (a full blow is a swing at full attack strength: the server's rule today):
   - shortsword, prop sabre: a quick diagonal cut, mirrored on every second swing; the sabre with a fencer's flick.
   - dagger and throwing knife in the hand: a short straight stab.
   - cleaver, hatchet: a chop from above. Mace: a heavier arc that swings through.
   - greatsword: a wide two-handed sweep. Maul: an overhead smash with both hands that ends low, with a small dip of the camera on a full blow that connects (off with reduced motion).
   - halberd, boom pole: a thrust at reach; the full blow a sweep.
   - the full blow of every class is told from a tap: a longer wind-up, a bigger arc, a shimmer that runs along the blade while attack strength is full (MODELS left the shimmer out).
   The clip's length follows the weapon's attack speed. Spamming, switching the slot in mid swing, swinging in the air, under water and while sneaking all look right.
3. Swing arcs and impact: a swing leaves a short arc of a few quads in the weapon's rarity colour (element colour if it has one), fading in about six ticks; a hit that connects gives a small spark where it landed, a bigger one on a critical hit; a miss gives none. Off with reduced motion; a client option for arcs. No per-frame allocation.
4. Shields (the owner named shields): the raised shield is in the picture since the fixes of MODELS (/home/user/shots/MODELS/review/S_shields.png): keep that. Raising it is a motion, not a switch: it comes up in three or four frames. A blocked hit kicks the shield back and flashes its rim; the riot shield's lamp flares. Third person: a braced stance.
5. Ranged: the release of the bow with a string snap and follow-through (the draw and the poses are the game's since the fixes); the crossbow's recoil; the knife throw with a wind-up and follow-through of the arm, and the knife seen in flight (take the picture MODELS missed).
6. Casting: a flick for the wand, the staff raised (both hands for a long cast); the head glows in the colour of the school of the spell selected on the spell bar and flares at the cast; a gesture of the free hand when a spell is cast without a focus.
7. Carrying, as the lead saw it in the sheets: a staff is carried upright like a walking staff (today it is couched like a lance: sheet 3_held_mannequin.png, m_staff_*); in first person a two-handed weapon is in view (today the maul is a corner of grey at the screen's edge and the halberd a few pixels: sheet 1_first_person.png), with the off hand on the grip if the game's first-person arm allows it (say what you could do).
8. What MODELS left open:
   - the icon of every unique shows the unique's own shape (today a recoloured base sprite: the gavel's icon is a purple ball on a stick). Paint them by script after the models' silhouettes, in the style of the other icons; the base gear keeps its icons.
   - the repaint of the other icons (supplies, bombs, crates, keys, treats, coins, quest items; sheet 10_icons_dark_light.png): one light from the upper left, three or four steps of shading, a dark outline that is not black, no single-pixel noise. A sheet before and after on dark and light slots.
   - the charm at the belt and the badge on the chest when slotted; rings, charms and badges with small world models; coins on the floor as little stacks that grow with the amount.
   - costumes on baby humanoids: none are drawn since the fixes (decided by the lead: as the game does for equipment without a baby layer). Leave it.
9. Proof. A still does not prove motion: add what the crawltest needs to take a strip (a picture every tick or every second tick through a clip; "tick freeze" and "tick step" exist in the game) and make strips for every class: light and full swing, block and blocked hit, draw and release, throw, cast, in first and in third person, and one strip of two players seeing each other (tools/crawltest/mp.sh). Look at every strip frame by frame: wind-up readable, no jump at the start or the end, the hit frame where the server's hit is. Sheets to /home/user/shots/__ID__/. Game tests: the clip chosen per class and situation, clip length from attack speed, that damage and timing of hits are what they were (the balance model must give the same numbers: tools/dev/balance.sh).
10. Docs: docs/ITEMS-ART.md (how to add a clip; the strips as the quality bar), docs/CONFIG.md (client options), docs/reports/__ID__.md.

Keep what works: item ids, components, numbers, saved worlds, the models and armour shapes of MODELS, the sounds (add a whoosh per class only where the swing has none).
