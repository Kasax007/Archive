**Belege** (geprüft am 13.11.2026):

- Projektseite https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/tnp-limitless-8: „Limitless 8 has just about 910 mods right now.“, „For the quests, there are currently 36 chapters with around 2245 quests total combined through all chapters.“, „Comments are disabled due to it being used for the wrong purpose.“ Autor Gbergz (Team TNP).
- Dateien (NeoForge 1.21.1): v1.79.0 (5.11.2026), 1.78.1 (27.10.), 1.77.0 (16.10.), 1.76.0 (9.10.), 1.75.0 (2.10.), 1.74.0 (25.9.) – fast jede Woche eine Version; 3.369 Downloads auf 5 Dateien in 41 Tagen, geschätzt ~2.500 im Monat.
- Mod-Liste v1.79.0 (913 Mods, aus der Pack-Datei gelesen): u. a. FTB Quests, Tropicraft, Small Ships, Create Aeronautics, Aquaculture 2, Fish of Thieves, When Dungeons Arise - Seven Seas, Critters and Companions, Doggy Talents Next. Kein Floß-Mod und keine Ozean-Dimension im Stil von Raft – für Beach Craft eine Teil-Lücke.

## Text (Discord-Vorschlagskanal, Englisch)

That is a huge amount of care. Your GitHub says suggestions belong here, so this is one from a fellow mod author.

> I make Beach Craft, which now also runs on NeoForge 1.21.1: the open-sea survival of Raft in Minecraft. A driftwood portal leads to the Endless Sea, a dimension of its own, where you build a raft block by block, sail it with mast, sails and motor, hook debris, visit story places and fight bosses. It would sit next to Tropicraft and Small Ships rather than replace them. If you'd like to try it, I'm happy to help: I could write a quest chapter for it or adjust configs and tags to your progression. Just tell me!
