This is a game show: it should have games. You add rooms that are not a fight (or a fight with a rule and a prize), generated into every floor, so that a run alternates between fighting, looting and playing. Reports to read: A1-world and B8-stagedoor (layout, room kinds, how a module claims rooms: Stations, Spots, RoomPainters), B9-rules (objectives, fight modifiers, shrines), A5-show (Emcee occasions, Hype, achievements), A9-sponsors, F8-floor (the wheel and the wager altars of floor 8: do not duplicate them, build on them), the report of part 1 of this task (module setpiece: searching, set pieces, gadgets) and UI1 (kit; docs/UI.md is binding). THE FRAME (decided): a new room kind "Bonus Round" that the layout hands out on every floor (two to four per floor, more on big floors; never on the critical path: a crawler in a hurry loses nothing; marked on the map with their own symbol once seen from the door). A bonus round is entered by stepping through a tinsel curtain; the Emcee names the game and its rule in one line (register lines for the voice task); a podium with a big button starts it; it can be played once per crawler (solo games) or once per cast (cast games: everyone present plays together and the prize scales). Losing never kills by itself and never soft-locks: a lost game opens the curtain again. Prizes come from one table per game and floor (crates, coins, Fans, a perk token if the builds task has merged, a cosmetic): docs/BALANCE.md gets the expected value per floor, tuned so that playing every bonus round is worth about as much as one more district of fights.

DELIVER at least ten games, each with its own built room (art by script, the floor's theme reskins the walls), rule, feedback (sounds, lights, a scoreboard block on the wall that shows the score live) and three prize tiers (bronze, silver, gold). Suggestions; replace any with a better idea of your own, but keep the spread (reflex, aim, memory, movement, nerve, knowledge, cooperation):
1. Hit Your Marks (memory, movement): floor marks light up in a sequence that grows by one each round; walk it without a wrong step.
2. Shooting Gallery (aim): pop-up flats on rails for bow, thrown knives and spells; friendly flats cost points; sixty seconds.
3. The Drop (nerve): a tall board of pegs; three tokens to drop from a walkway; the slots below hold prizes and one boot.
4. Quiz Podium (knowledge): three questions about this run's dungeon, generated from what the crawler has met (which monster does what, what the floor's rule is, what a stat does), four buzzers; a right answer teaches as much as it pays. Questions and answers as data.
5. Hot Seat (nerve): a growing pot; press the button to add a round with a rising chance of a harmless but loud bust that loses the pot; cash out at any time.
6. Stunt Course (movement): a short obstacle run over moving platforms, swinging bags and a rope swing against a clock, with medal times; the best time of the cast is kept on the wall.
7. Spotlight Stand (fight with a rule): hold the lit circle for forty-five seconds while monsters pour in; the circle moves.
8. Prop Auction (cooperation and coins): three covered lots; the cast bids coins blind; the lots are revealed; one of them is always a joke.
9. Wire Room (puzzle): a fuse board of coloured cables to patch by a diagram that is torn in half between two walls; cast mates can each read one half.
10. Lights, Camera (reflex): a wall of lamps; hit the lit one before it goes out; speed rises; combo counter.
11. Statues (movement): cross the room while the director puppet has its back turned; move while it looks and start again.
12. Double or Nothing door: after any won game, a second curtain: keep the prize or fight an elite for double.
Also: Audience Challenges: a button at the door of some ordinary combat rooms (about one in six; never the only way through) that arms a challenge for that room before the fight starts: under a time, without being hit, with the set only (part 1's set pieces), without leaving a circle; shown as a chip in the floor panel; a won challenge pays Hype and a small crate, a lost one a rim shot.

Requirements for all of them: playable alone and by a cast of up to eight; fair at every latency a normal server has (the server judges; the client only shows); saved state per crawler and cast (played, best score), safe across relog and restart in the middle (the game ends as lost without a penalty); no way to leave the cell or to trap a crawler; monsters of the floor never enter a bonus round except where the game calls them. Scores go to the recap and the Hall of Fame board (a small "games won" line) and into six achievements.
