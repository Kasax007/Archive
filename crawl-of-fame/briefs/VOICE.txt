THE CONTRACT WITH THE LEAD (fixed: the lead's synthesis reads and writes exactly this)
- The spoken script lives in tools/content/voice/script/<speaker>.json: {"speaker": "emcee", "lines": [{"id": "boss_kill.0", "text": "And the boss is down! Take a bow, crawler: the arena lights are all yours.", "how": "triumphant, rising, ends on a shout", "for": ["crawloffame.show.line.boss_kill.0"]}]}. "text" is what is spoken: plain English, numbers written out, no placeholders, no names of players; tags in square brackets for sounds a voice makes are allowed and only these: [laugh] [chuckle] [sigh] [gasp] [cough] [clear throat] [groan] [sniff] [shush]. "how" is the direction for the performance, written as a note to an actor in one sentence (mood, pace, where it rises, what the last words do): the speech model is given the speaker's description from the cast and this note, and performs the line accordingly, so the note decides how the line sounds. Vary it: the same note on forty lines gives forty identical deliveries. "for" lists the lang keys of the displayed lines this recording belongs to (several displayed variants may share one recording; one displayed line may have recordings per argument: see below).
- The cast lives in tools/content/voice/cast.json: per speaker id a display name, a description of the voice in one or two sentences (age, pitch, texture, accent, temperament: the lead designs the voice from it), and the processing preset ("pa" for voices that come over the studio's loudspeakers, "earpiece", "room" for people who stand in front of the player, "tv").
- Audio files: src/main/modules/voice/assets/crawloffame/sounds/voice/<speaker>/<id with dots as underscores>.ogg, mono, 24 kHz, Vorbis; an index written by the script at assets/crawloffame/voice/index.json (id -> speaker, file, milliseconds, the lang keys) that the client reads; sound events voice.<speaker>.<id> in the module's sounds.json, every one with "stream": true (a hundred minutes of speech must never sit decoded in memory) and without a vanilla subtitle (the game shows the text itself).
- tools/content/voice/build.py (yours): validates the script (ids unique, text speakable: no digits, no %, no stray symbols, under 28 words unless marked "long": true; every "for" key exists in a lang file; only known tags), writes sounds.json and the index for the files that exist, and lists what is missing. tools/content/voice/draft.py (yours): makes a placeholder recording for every line that has no file yet, so that the game side can be built and tested before the real voices arrive: use the small open speech model the lead installed (run: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/e2686f39-d632-56d3-aa9e-a2a2f832009c/scratchpad/voice/draft.sh <text file> <out wav> [voice] if that file exists: about two seconds of computing per second of speech, a text file of lines "id<TAB>text" writes one wav per id into a directory; otherwise a spoken-rhythm tone pattern of the right length from tools/content/audio/dsp.py). Draft files are written beside the final ones with the suffix .draft.ogg and are replaced by a final file of the same id; the index says which is which. Do not commit more than 40 draft files (enough for the tests and the tour): the lead commits the real ones.

DELIVER
1. The spoken script, the larger half of this task. Go through every text of the mod that somebody says and write its spoken form; say in the report how many lines per speaker and the total word count (the lead plans the synthesis with it: aim for 900 to 1100 lines and about 10000 words in all: the speech model needs about eight seconds of computing per second of speech, so every line costs; if the mod has more, voice the best and leave the rest as text, and mark each line with "tier": 1, 2 or 3 (1 = heard by every player in the first hour, 3 = rare) so that the lead records in that order).
   - The Emcee (speaker "emcee"; the star: give him the most and the best): every occasion in show (run start, floor arrival per floor: write dedicated arrival lines for each of the ten floors instead of voicing templates with numbers; kills, deaths by cause, boss kills, countdown and clock, Hype, fans, achievements of the rare kinds, patch notes, dead air, crate moments, set strike), the rule lines of the ten floors, boss introductions and phase lines of every boss (these name the boss: voice them as written), the win and the credits. Lines that show a player's name are spoken without it ("our crawler", "one of tonight's cast", "the newcomer"): write them so that they sound natural, not like a gap. Where an argument has few values (a floor, a boss, a role, a rarity) you may write one recording per value: "for" then carries the key and the value ("crawloffame.show.line.floor_arrive.2#floor=4").
   - The Gaffer (speaker "gaffer"): his dialogue, the hints, and the orientation and earpiece lines if that task has merged.
   - The people: every named person of npc (vendors, inhabitants, quest givers: about two dozen; each is a speaker of their own with a description that fits their texts and their look), their greetings, offers, thanks, shop barks (buying, selling, cannot afford, browsing too long), and the short answers of the player are never spoken.
   - The host of the interviews and the sponsor reads (speaker "host" and "announcer_ads"), the voice of the Network for rules and penalties (speaker "network": cold, corporate, pleased with itself), the sidekick trainer if the module has speech.
   - New barks where the show is silent today and a voice would add life (write them, register them as occasions): the Emcee on a long kill streak, a narrow escape (health under a tenth, then safe), a crawler standing still for a minute in a combat room, the first rare drop, a cast wipe barely avoided; vendors calling out when a crawler walks past without stopping. Each occasion at least four variants: a line heard twice in ten minutes is a line too few.
   Rules for the writing: our own words (tools/dev/check_names.py), no references to real shows, people or brands; funny in the dry way the existing lines are; every line speakable in under eight seconds unless it is a boss introduction or a speech; a direction in "how" that an actor could follow.
2. Playback on the client (client.voice.VoiceDirector), category SoundSource.VOICE (the game's own "Voice/Speech" slider):
   - The server keeps sending what it sends today (lang key and arguments); the client finds the recording through the index (key, then key#argument=value, then nothing: text only). No new payload unless an occasion has none.
   - One voice at a time per channel: the PA (Emcee, Network, host, ads) is not positional and never talks over itself; people are positional at the speaker's place and one person speaks at a time; the earpiece (Gaffer's remote lines) is not positional. A PA line and a person may overlap, the PA line is then lowered by 8 dB.
   - A queue with sense: every line has a priority (boss introduction and phase, death, floor arrival, win: high; kills, Hype, fans: normal; dead air, patch notes, idle remarks: low), a time to live (a kill remark that could not start within 4 s is dropped; high ones wait up to 15 s), a minimum gap of 1.2 s between lines, and a higher line stops a low one with a 150 ms fade. Never the same recording twice within ten minutes if another variant exists (remember recent ones per occasion).
   - The banner (and the dialogue screen, the lower third, the toast) shows the text for as long as its recording plays, never shorter than today; text and voice start together. In the dialogue screen the person's line plays when the page opens, stops with a short fade when the player clicks on, and the text can still be read at the player's own speed.
   - While a voice plays, music is lowered by 8 dB and ambience by 4 dB with 250 ms ramps, and comes back after it.
   - Client options (the kit's settings corner and config/crawloffame-client.json): voices on/off, "Emcee talks: everything / important only / never", people's voices on/off, subtitles for voices always on (they are the existing texts).
   - Missing file, missing index entry, sound device absent: text only, one debug line, never an error.
3. A client option: Music = "Fights and big moments" (default) / "Always" (today's behaviour) / "Off". Log every change as today.
