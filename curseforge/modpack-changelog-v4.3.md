## Challenge Craft 26.3-v4.3 - Mod update

Mod update: Challenge Craft 1.1.5 (the pack had 1.1.3).

- **Infinite Chest:** what you take out or put in by clicking is now always saved. Before, a reload could bring taken
  items back or lose items you put in.
- **Infinite Chest:** a left click takes one stack of that item (one sword, 16 ender pearls), no longer 64. Only the
  chest you have open and in reach reacts to your clicks.
- **Crafter:** no longer crafts the Infinite Chest or the casino devices. They keep their level and purchase
  requirements, so craft them by hand.
- **Dice:** you keep your rolled blocks when someone joins a LAN world or the challenge settings change.
- **Challenge selection:** the count next to each section (like "3/7") updates as soon as you switch a challenge on
  or off.
- The thrown die shows its name.

### Pack
- The game window is named "Challenge Craft 26.3". It still said 26.2.

Worlds and progress are kept.

### Server
- The server pack contains Challenge Craft 1.1.5.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/challengecraft-*.jar.
