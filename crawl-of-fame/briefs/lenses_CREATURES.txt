motion: every creature's clips, frame by frame
The owner asked for good animations, excellent textures and variety; the bar is that a player reads a wind-up before the hit and tells every kind from every other as a black shape at ten blocks. Look at every strip and sheet in /home/user/shots/__ID__/ frame by frame and take your own where one is missing (the crawltest's strip command, 1280x720 at GUI 2, survival, default brightness; also one fight against a pack of six and one boss fight from the player's eyes at 960x540 GUI 2). For every reworked kind: idle (does it breathe, shift its weight, have a habit of its own, or stand like a statue), walk and run (do the feet slide, does the body carry weight, does stride follow speed), the attack's three beats (a wind-up a player can read, the strike where the hit lands, a recovery; name the clip and the frame where it snaps, slides or jumps), each ability's clip, hurt, stunned, the death (its own, with its particles and sound, no red tip-over), secondary motion on what hangs, blending when one clip starts during another, a pack that moves in step. Models: a silhouette of its own per kind (the black-shape line-up), hands that hold the tool, jaws, clothing as parts. Textures at arm's length and at ten blocks beside the old sheets: one light direction, shading in steps, readable faces, no speckle, the faction's palette with one accent, glow layers. Bosses: the pose for the title card, the act change on the body, the death scene and what the arena does. Whatever looks like its neighbour, like a box, or like the old sine swing is a finding: name the kind, the clip and the frame.

breaks: animation in a real game
Try to break it: two clients seeing the same monsters (tools/crawltest/mp.sh): do both see the same clip at the same moment; a client that joins, relogs or comes into range in the middle of a wind-up, a stun, an act change or a death; a monster that dies, is stunned, frozen, pushed, teleported or unloaded in the middle of its wind-up (does the blow still land, on whom, where); a blow that started lands where it was aimed (step away: does stepping away work for every brute and boss, on a laggy client too); forty monsters in a hall (frame time with and without the task: say how you measured; allocation per frame in the animation code: read it); server cost (tools/dev/load.sh 4 for two places against the numbers in the places' reports); the balance model (tools/dev/balance.sh at depths 1, 5 and 9 for the reworked casts against docs/BALANCE.md: what moved and why); hitboxes, abilities, names and saved worlds as they were (a world saved before the task loads with monsters in mid fight); spectators, the bestiary or any screen that draws a monster, the boss title card and the line-up card; people and helpers (they share the model: do vendors still stand at their counters and face the customer, do dialogue portraits still fit); a resource pack reload in mid clip; the production jar (tools/dev/prod.sh: no error, no missing clip or texture); the other engineer's places built in the meantime (does a kind without clips still animate sensibly). One vocabulary of motion: is the easing of the held-item clips reused, or are there now two. Write scratch tests under /tmp and report what fails.
