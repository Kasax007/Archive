Summary of the lead (the change is on the branch: commit 3c7fc8b "Floor card: steps aside into a caption while the Emcee reads it"; docs/UI.md, "One panel at a time", the two last points). Why: in the golden path's pictures (/home/user/shots/GOLDENPATH, golden2_path_00 to _04) the floor card stayed in the middle of the view with the screen dimmed until the Emcee had read his arrival line, the place's rule and what the depth brings (10 to 30 seconds; Hollowmere Manor's rule alone takes the voice 17 s), and the crawler walked out of the entry room and fought a room under it. What changed: (1) client.show.FloorCard: the card holds the view for its own SHOW_MS (4.6 s), then slides out (the slide-out runs in the card's last 450 ms, the dim lifts in its last 500 ms) and, when the card's voice reads on for at least CAPTION_MIN_MS (1.5 s) longer, a caption takes its place at the Emcee's banner's row and width (EmceeBanner.topRow, EmceeBanner.span): a gold stripe, the microphone, "FLOOR n" and the place's name as a head line, a hairline, then a page of the part the voice reads (the rule in the text colour; each service in gold with its bullet). Pages are whole sentences, at most PAGE_LINES (3) lines (one long sentence up to 5), made per part when the caption first draws (and again for another width); a page begins at its first character's share of its part and is shown when the voice's progress reaches it (client.voice.VoiceDirector.progress: 0 to 1 while that text's line plays, else -1; followed in the client tick). Before the first part plays (the arrival line speaks) the first page shows; after a part, its last page stays until the next begins. The caption ends with the voice in real time (captionEnd = max(start + 1.5 s, the voice's end + 1.2 s)): a screen opened meanwhile hides it but does not hold it back. (2) Under the caption: FloorCard.visible() is false (the minimap is back), due() is false (toasts may start), covers() returns the caption's rectangle (banners wait or pass as under a card; toasts under it wait), toastCeiling() puts the toast stack below it where it reaches into the toasts' column, showing() is true (client.orientation.GafferLine.emceeHolds now uses it: the Gaffer waits for the Emcee as before). (3) client.show.EmceeBanner: topRow() and span() are shared with the caption; in the wide case the banner keeps SLIDE_PX (12) clear of the toast column, because it slides 12 px out to the right (the layers script caught a banner going out five pixels over a toast for three frames, case D). (4) Crawltest: hcheck "card" (the big card is on screen), "caption" (the caption is on screen); "nocard" now fails while anything of the card is left (FloorCard.active()).
