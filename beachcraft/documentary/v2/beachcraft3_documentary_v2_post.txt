TITLE (suggestion)
Beach Craft 3.1 - Tides of the Deep: a calm documentary of the whole mod (with shaders)

DESCRIPTION
A slow, calm walk through everything in Beach Craft 3.1 "Tides of the Deep" for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric),
filmed with shaders (Iris + Complementary Reimagined): the biomes of the Endless Sea with day and night, all nineteen
structures and their loot, every creature, the blocks and items and how they work, building your own raft and meeting
the people of the sea, and at the end the whole story from the castaway chest to the fight against the Leviathan.

Chapters:
00:00 The Endless Sea
00:38 The Way In
01:18 The World
04:24 The Places
07:59 The Creatures
10:22 Blocks And Items
14:53 Your Own Raft
15:36 People Of The Sea
18:13 The Story
