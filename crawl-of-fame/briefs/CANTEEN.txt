TASK __ID__: the canteen: a cook who feeds the cast, and somewhere to sit. The owner of the mod wrote: "Can we also have the show automatically generated and being aired on a big TV in the green rooms or kitchens where NPCs can give me food? This would be fun." This task builds the kitchen half of that wish (the television is its own task, BROADCAST, and hangs on the wall you leave for it): a crawler who reaches a green room is handed a plate by somebody with a temper, sits down and eats it. Green rooms are the show's backstage pocket and look the same in every place (docs/PLACES.md); what is on the plate comes from the place the green room stands in. Reports to read: THEMES1 and docs/PLACES.md (place and depth), A2-run (green rooms, members, floors), B5-dressing, B7-dressing23 and B8-stagedoor (the green room compositions: RoomPainters.lounge, Spots and Stations), A8-npc (people, dialogue, shops, how a person is placed and drawn), ROOMS1 if it exists (searching: the craft-service trolley and what it gives), B3-items and ITEMS (supplies, consumables, the item card), B2-profile (stats and the timed bonuses that exist), SIDEKICK, A5-show (Emcee occasions), UI1 and docs/UI.md (kit; one panel at a time; client.ui.Layers), docs/ITEMS-ART.md and MODELS2 (clips for hands and held items), docs/CREATURES.md if it exists (a person who moves is built to that standard), docs/VOICE.md (spoken lines get a direction in the voice script; the lead records them). A new module "canteen" (net.kasax.crawl.canteen, client.canteen, resources src/main/modules/canteen, scripts tools/content/canteen).

THE BAR: the cook is somebody a player remembers and quotes. Eating is something the crawler does, with a plate in the hand, not a click on a menu. A plate is a decision about the next floor, said in one line. The kitchen looks used: steam, a bell, pans, a specials board that changes.

DELIVER
1. The kitchenette: every green room gets a counter with a hatch and a bell, an urn, a fridge and a specials board (the show's own art, one set for all places, by script to the standard of the common rules; placed by the green room compositions through a hook in world, listed under shared changes; nothing may block the booth, the vendors, the stairs or a Spot). Leave one wall free for the big screen of the later task: a named Spot "screen wall" of at least 4x3 blocks with the seats facing it; until that task lands a show poster hangs there.
2. The cook: from depth 2 the larger green rooms are a canteen with the cook behind the hatch: a person of our own with a name, a body and a face of their own, an idle that is busy (stirs, wipes, tastes, looks up when a crawler comes near), a temper, and about forty spoken lines with directions in the voice script (greeting by mood, the plate handed over, remarks on what the crawler took last time, on a crawler who is hurt, on a sidekick, on a cast that arrives without one of its members, on a crawler who rings the bell too often). At depth 1 and in small green rooms the hatch is shut with a note, and the fridge and the urn still work.
3. The plate: once per green room visit the cook offers every crawler three dishes of the day (kit screen: three cards with the dish's picture, its name, one clear sentence, how long it lasts), one of which is always a dish of the place the green room stands in. A dish is a clear bonus for the next ten minutes or the next floor (healing over time, speed, coin find, a longer breath of Hype, resistance to the place's rule): at least twelve common dishes and two per place that exists (data per place; docs/PLACES.md gets a section that tells every later place how to add its two). The three are fixed per visit and crawler; the cook remembers what was taken. docs/BALANCE.md gets a paragraph: what a plate is worth, and that the bands of the fights hold with and without one.
4. Eating: the dish is a plate in the hand (item model and a clip of the clip system: raise, eat in three bites, an empty plate), seen by other players; eating takes three seconds, can be done standing or sitting, and is interrupted by a hit. The fridge gives one snack per floor, the urn a coffee (a short haste and a remark of the cook). The sidekick gets a bowl beside its crawler's seat and eats when the crawler does.
5. Specials: the cook cooks from ingredients a crawler brings (what searching gives, if ROOMS1 exists; add six ingredients of your own to loot otherwise and say so): a short list of recipes as data, found by trying; a tried combination that is no recipe gives an honest "house stew" (a weak bonus), never nothing; a found recipe is kept per player across runs in a recipe card of the kit and marked on the specials board.
6. Seats: sofas and canteen chairs a crawler can sit on (use to sit, sneak to stand; a seat faces the screen wall; no way to sit into a wall or to push somebody out of a seat). A meal eaten sitting lasts a fifth longer ("Feet up"); leave the hook the screen task needs (who sits and where they look) and name it.
7. The show notices: three lines of the Emcee (a first meal, a found recipe, a crawler who skips every meal for three floors), four achievements.
8. Tests: game tests for the offer (three dishes fixed per visit, one of the place, once per visit, saved across relog and restart), each dish's bonus applied and ended, eating interrupted, the fridge and the urn (once per floor), recipes (found, kept across runs, the stew), seats (sit, stand, relog while sitting, a seat whose block is gone), the sidekick's bowl, a cast of two (each their own plate), placement in green rooms of every size (the screen wall free, nothing blocked), packets checked (a plate asked for from another room, a dish that was not offered). Crawltest scripts in survival: a green room with the kitchenette at depth 1 and the canteen at depths 2 and 5 in two places (the dish of the place differs), the offer screen on 960x540 and 854x480 at GUI 2 and on 480x270 GUI pixels, a strip through eating standing and sitting in first and third person, the cook's idle as a strip, the recipe card, the specials board; a listening run with the cook's lines (tools/crawltest/listen.py: the cook is understood, the Emcee does not talk over the cook). Sheets to /home/user/shots/__ID__/. Look at them. If tools/dev/golden.sh exists (docs/TESTING.md), give the golden path a stage: take a plate and eat it at one green room.
