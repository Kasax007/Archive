   - pyro crates: explode when hit or shot, chain to neighbours, set oil alight; marked clearly; hurt crawlers too;
   - oil drums that spill a slick when knocked over (slows, burns when lit), water mains that flood a patch (conducts shock spells and the fuse box's sparks);
   - a fuse box that overloads the room's floor lamps for three seconds when pulled (shocks everything standing on metal grating; a cooldown of a minute);
   - a stage trapdoor with a lever (opens under what stands on it; monsters fall into the pit below and climb out hurt after a while, or die on spikes on later floors);
   - a safety curtain on a chain that splits a room in two for twenty seconds;
   - a spotlight on a stand that can be turned and switched on: blinds monsters in its cone, and whoever fights in the light gains Hype faster;
   - a wind machine (pushes monsters and projectiles down a lane), a fog machine (monsters lose track, crawlers too);
   - the traps that exist (rules.trap) become usable on purpose: a lever or a shot sets them off while monsters stand in them.
