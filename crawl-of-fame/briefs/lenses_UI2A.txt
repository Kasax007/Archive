Play it in survival at 480x270 GUI pixels (960x540 GUI 2) and at the five other sizes of the tour (a script of your own beside the task's; default brightness): arriving on a floor (card, banner, hint, toasts: one after the other, or on each other), a fight in Hollowmere Manor with a sidekick, a boss with its bar, a death, the recap, the High Roller Hall's wheel, the season's line-up.
