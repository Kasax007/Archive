TASK __ID__: builds, part 1: a technique for every weapon. The owner of the mod wrote: "I love having many different things to try and builds to test, also throughout the runs" and "weapons are hard to understand". Today the weapon types differ in three numbers (damage, speed, reach) and, since the task MODELS2, in how their swing looks; nothing a weapon does can be called a move. After this task every weapon type has a move of its own that a player learns in one use, and a crawler can measure what a build does. THE BAR: a player who presses the technique key with a weapon they have never held understands what it did without reading, wants to do it again, and would pick the weapon for it. Each of the seventeen feels different in the hands by timing alone. Fewer effects made excellent beat many that are a damage number with a new name.

1. A technique for every weapon type: one active move on a new key ("Technique", default R; if R is taken by the spell bar or another of the mod's keys, find the right key and say why), shown beside the spell bar as a slot of the kit with the technique's icon, a cooldown sweep and the key hint, and one passive trait. Every technique has a wind-up a player can feel (a clip with wind-up, strike and recovery, first and third person, a sound of its own, a mark on the floor where it will land if it lands on an area), a clear effect, and fits the type and its numbers. Suggestions (improve freely, keep the spread): shortsword: a lunge forward, a guaranteed crit on a target that is winding up / balanced: no penalty anywhere; cleaver: a chop that opens a wound (damage over a few seconds) / more damage to wounded targets; mace: an overhead blow that staggers / ignores part of armour; dagger: a dash through the target to its back / crits from behind; hatchet: thrown, sticks, comes back when recalled / faster after a kill; prop sabre: a parry stance for half a second, a riposte if hit in it / more damage after a block; greatsword: a wide sweep through everything in front / hits two more targets at reduced damage; maul: a ground slam in a circle that staggers / knocks down small monsters; halberd: a hooking pull that drags a monster in and trips it / the first hit on an approaching target is stronger; boom pole: a vault over a monster or a gap, with a landing blow / keeps monsters at reach; shortbow: a volley of three / faster draw while moving; crossbow: a bolt that goes through a line / the first shot after a reload crits; throwing knife: a fan of five / knives can be picked up again; wand: an instant cast of the selected spell at half cost / spells cast faster; staff: a channelled beam of the selected spell's school / more mana; buckler: a bash that interrupts a monster's wind-up / a block within a few ticks of the hit reflects; riot shield: a braced advance that pushes a lane clear / blocks projectiles for mates behind. Uniques keep their type's technique; where a unique's text promises a move of its own, give it one. Techniques scale with the weapon's Power and a stat that suits the type, train the weapon's skill, cost nothing but the cooldown (melee, ranged) or mana (foci), work the same for every member of a cast and against bosses within stated limits (no stun during an introduction or an act change; say per technique what a boss ignores), and are decided on the server: a packet proves nothing.
