For every beat: is it obvious what to do next from what the world shows (the lit thing, the marker, the key hint) and the tracker line, without reading a wall of text; does the key hint show the key that is really bound (rebind one and look); is what the Gaffer says true to what the game does (check each claim about stats, weapons, crates, the stash, the map, Hype against the code); is every line of his short enough to be spoken in under eight seconds, in his own voice, without naming a place or a floor number, without a joke about being a tutorial, and present in the voice script with a direction (tools/content/voice/build.py must end without an error); does anything overlap, clip or get cut at the three sizes (tracker, lower third with his portrait, markers); does he walk without sliding or teleporting in view.
