script: the spoken script, before eighty-five minutes of it are recorded
Every line of tools/content/voice/script/*.json will be performed by a speech model that is given the speaker's description (cast.json) and the line's direction ("how") and costs about eight seconds of computing per second of speech, so a line that is wrong is expensive: find them now. Read ALL 799 lines (write a small script under /tmp that prints them by speaker with their directions and the displayed text of their "for" keys beside them). Report, with the line ids: lines that cannot be spoken as written (digits, symbols, abbreviations, a placeholder or a player's name left in, a tag in square brackets that is not on the allowed list, a word a speech model will stumble on that has a plain synonym); spoken texts that say something else than the displayed text they belong to, or something the game does not do; a spoken variant without the player's name that sounds like a gap instead of a sentence; directions that are missing, that contradict the text, that ask for what a voice cannot do, or that repeat (list groups of lines of one speaker with the same or nearly the same direction: forty identical directions give forty identical deliveries); speakers whose description does not fit their texts or their look in the game, or two speakers with descriptions so alike that they will sound the same; lines for a place that name a floor number; tiers that are wrong (a line every player hears in the first hour marked 3, a rare boss line marked 1); anything that is not our own (a name, a catch phrase or a joke from a real show, film, game or person). Also count: lines over 28 words not marked long, occasions with fewer than three variants that fire often. This lens is reading and judging; it needs no Minecraft run.

playback: the game side of the voices and the new music rule
Read client.voice (VoiceDirector, the queue, the options), voice.VoiceQueue and voice.Barks, the ducking in client.audio and the music modes of MusicDirector, and the places that were changed to start and hold a voice (banner, floor card, dialogue, interview, toasts, shop). Look for: two voices at once on a channel, a line that never ends its hold (a banner or toast that stays for ever when the sound is missing, muted or the device is absent), a queue that grows without bound or keeps playing after a disconnect, a world change or a death, state kept in statics across worlds, sound events that are not streamed (a hundred minutes of speech decoded into memory), a recording looked up by the wrong key or argument (the place of an arrival line), the options not saved or wiping other keys of the client config, the volume slider not applied, a person's voice heard by players who are not in that dialogue (decide from the code what happens and say whether that is right), music that does not come back after ducking or plays while exploring in the default mode, boss, last-minutes, strike and victory music broken by the new modes, the fight signal (the monster's aggressive flag) changing how any monster looks or behaves, barks firing for the wrong crawler or after the run ended. Prove with the queue's tests, with scratch tests under /tmp and with the log of tools/crawltest/scripts/voice.txt where you need it.
