**Belege** (geprüft am 9.11.2026):

- Projektseite https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/better-oneblock: Start mit einem einzigen OneBlock, das neue Blöcke und Phasen freischaltet; geplant sind laut Seite „Monster Party“, Phase 11 und 12, persönliche One Blocks und Elite-Mobs; ohne Struktur-Mods. Kategorien Skyblock, Map Based, Vanilla+.
- Neueste Datei „Better OneBlock-v5.0.2“ (Fabric 1.21.1, 4.11.2026); v5.0.1 vom 25.10.2026 hat 16.940 Downloads, v4.7.1 vom 10.10.2026 22.045. Schätzung: rund 40.000 Downloads im Monat.
- Mod-Liste der Datei 5.0.2 (175 Mods): u. a. OneBlock Mod, Advancements Reloaded, Clickable advancements, Supplementaries, Handcrafted, Friends&Foes, Mutant Monsters, EMI. Kein Questbuch (kein FTB Quests, Heracles, Odyssey, Better Questing).
- Issue-Vorlage https://github.com/razvangn/Better-OneBlock-Modpack (Datei `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/suggestion.md`, Label „suggestion“).

## Text (GitHub-Issue, Englisch)

> Hi navzary! Better OneBlock is such a fun take on OneBlock – I love how every phase opens up new blocks, and that Phases 11 and 12, personal One Blocks and elite mobs are already on the way. Your page says to share any suggestions, so here's mine: a quest book could give players a clear path through the phases, with a little reward for finishing each one.
>
> I make Quest Icons, an FTB-Quests-style quest book for Fabric 1.21.1 (and 26.x): chapters, quest maps, tasks, rewards, loot crates and teams, plus an in-game editor with forms and pickers, so nobody has to write SNBT. If you'd like to give it a try, I'd love to write you a free starter chapter – one quest line per phase – and set it up for the pack. Just tell me what you need!
>
> https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quest-icons
