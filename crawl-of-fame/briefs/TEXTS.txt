TASK TEXTS (a review task of the lead, 14.11.): every text a player reads or hears is read by two independent reviewers (lenses: echoes of existing works; language as a copy editor and as a new player), and an engineer settles what they find. There was no build: this file is the task's text for the engineer who settles the findings.

THE RULE BEHIND IT: everything in the mod is original (docs/DESIGN.md section 2): no name, character, scene, catchphrase or quoted line of any book, game, film, series, song, play or real programme. Texts are British English in the show's voice (DESIGN section 9): the Emcee a dry showman, the Gaffer a tired stagehand, vendors with their patter.

HOW A TEXT IS CHANGED
- Most lang files are written by generators (tools/content/<module>/*.py); a text changed only in the lang file comes back at the next run. Change the generator and the lang file together, or run the generator and read git status: only the lines you meant may change (tools/content/world/blocks.py also redraws textures: nothing but your text may differ). A lang file without a generator is edited by hand; keep its order and indentation.
- A spoken line has its words twice: in tools/content/voice/script/<speaker>.json ("text", with the direction "how") and in the lang key or keys it is "for" (docs/VOICE.md: the contract). Change both; keep the direction true to the new words. The lead's recorder records a changed line again by itself and the lead publishes it; until then the line shows as text only. python3 tools/content/voice/build.py must end without an error after your change. Do not touch the recordings.
- A replacement is written fresh in the voice of its neighbours (read ten lines around it first), is as short as what it replaces, fits where it is shown (a title, a toast, a card: look at the screen's width in docs/UI.md) and passes tools/dev/check_names.py. Add every phrase that was caught to docs/NAMES_NOT_ALLOWED.txt (the section of phrases at its end).
- Words for the same thing (the second lens's part 2): correct a text that is plainly wrong by itself. A rename across the mod (what a player calls a floor, a place, a cast) is a decision of the lead: do not apply it; put the reviewer's table and your own recommendation under "open" in the report, with the number of texts and of recordings each choice would touch.
- Game tests that quote a text are changed with it. No code is expected to change otherwise; if a finding shows a text that is wrong because the game changed (a number, a key), fix the text from the code or the data, not the other way round.

REPORT: create docs/reports/TEXTS.md (at most 60 lines): what was read, each finding fixed (commit), refuted (why) or open (what the lead must decide), the phrases added to the list, the lines that need a new recording.

ACCEPTANCE: tools/dev/jc.sh; the full game tests; tools/dev/check_names.py; python3 tools/content/audio/check.py --quiet; python3 tools/content/voice/build.py without an error.
