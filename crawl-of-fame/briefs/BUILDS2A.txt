THE BAR: a player reads three cards and knows within five seconds which one they want and why, because each says in one sentence what will be different in their hands. No rewrite is "+5 % damage": each changes what the player does or when they do it. By the third pick of a run the player can name their build, and two runs in a row do not feel alike. Sixty rewrites that each do something beat ninety with thirty fillers.

An offer that is not answered waits (a small pulsing chip in the HUD with the key, on the layer rule; a count if several wait), never opens by itself in a fight, and survives relog, restart and death (the Remains keep nothing of it: say what a death does to picks, and why).
