TASK __ID__: stations: benches where a crawler makes something of what the dungeon gave. The owner of the mod asked for "functional blocks", "workbenches and stations" and more to do than fighting; docs/PLAN.md has carried "stations: Tinker's Bench, Alchemy Stand, Whetstone" as open since phase 2. Today loot is worn, sold or left; materials pile up in the stash with no use; nothing in a green room is operated by hand except the booth and the vendors. After this task a green room is a place where a crawler stands for a minute and decides things. Reports to read: B3-items (gear, affixes, rarities, item level, materials, supplies), B4-crates (stash, trinkets), the items-UX report (Power, the item card, the kit's decorator, locks and junk), A6-magic (spells, tomes, scrolls), B2-profile (skills: several crafting-like skills exist: read which, and which are unused), B8-stagedoor (Stations and Spots: how a module gets a block into rooms), A8-npc (shops), the report of "things to do" part 1 if merged (searching, materials and ingredients found in props), the report THEMES1 (place and depth: stations belong to green rooms, which are the same in every place), UI1 (kit; docs/UI.md is binding). A new module "craft" (net.kasax.crawl.craft, client.craft, resources src/main/modules/craft, scripts tools/content/craft).

THE IDEA (decided): four stations, each a block with art and a screen on the kit, each answering one question a crawler has in a green room. Every green room gets two of the four (fixed per room by its seed, so a cast learns where things are; the first green room of a run always has the Prop Bench), larger green rooms all four. Materials are the currency of all of them: the materials that exist plus what salvaging gives. Nothing here needs a recipe book: every screen shows what goes in, what comes out and what it costs before anything is spent, and every action is one click with a short animation and a sound of its own.

DELIVER
1. The Prop Bench (salvage and repair of a run's clutter): put in any gear: it comes apart into parts by rarity and item level (scrap, fittings, a rare component from rare gear and above, a spark from a unique), shown before the lever is pulled; "Salvage junk" takes everything marked as junk (items-UX) at once with a confirm that lists the yield. The bag's and stash's sell-or-leave problem ends here.
2. The Fitting Room (make a piece better): one piece on the stand: raise its item level towards the depth's level for parts (never above what this depth drops: it lets a favourite piece keep up, it does not outgrow the floor); reroll one affix of the crawler's choice while the others stay (the cost rises per reroll on that piece; the card shows the possible range before the roll); add an empty affix slot to a piece below its rarity's maximum for a rare component. The comparison card of items-UX shows before and after.
3. The Still (brew): three ingredient slots and a burner: combinations of the supplies and ingredients that exist make drinks with clear effects for the next minutes or the next floor (the potions that exist become recipes here; add eight new ones that a build would want: a crit tonic, a block draught, a mana syrup, a Hype fizz...). Recipes are found by trying and are then remembered in a small book on the screen (per player, across runs); a bad mix gives a harmless joke drink, never nothing. Trains the brewing skill that exists (or the nearest one: say which).
4. The Grindstone and Polish (prepare for the next fight): a quick buff station with a small skill game (hold while a needle swings; release in the green band): a sharpened edge (more damage for the next two rooms), a polished shield (better block), a tuned focus (cheaper spells); the better the release, the longer it holds; once per piece per green room. A minute of hands-on care that pays in the next fight.
5. Placement and feel: the stations as blocks of the show's backstage (they belong to the green room, not to the place: one set of art) placed by the green room compositions through world.Stations (a hook in world, listed under shared changes), each with a soft idle animation or light, a name plate when looked at with the kit's key hint, and a line of the Emcee the first time a crawler uses each (register for the voice task if tools/content/voice exists). A vendor sells parts dearly and buys them cheaply, so that materials have a price.
6. Economy: say in docs/BALANCE.md what a thorough crawler can afford per floor (parts in, upgrades out) and tune yields and costs so that the Fitting Room keeps about two pieces current per floor, not the whole set; no number of the fights changes.
7. Server and safety: every action is validated on the server (the piece is the crawler's, in reach of the station, the parts are there); nothing is lost or duplicated on relog, restart or a closed screen in the middle (game tests for each action with the screen closed halfway, a full bag, a locked piece, two crawlers at one station).
