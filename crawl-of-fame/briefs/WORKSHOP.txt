THE IDEA (decided): four stations, each a block with art and a screen on the kit, each answering one question a crawler has in a green room. Every green room gets two of the four (fixed per room by its seed, so a cast learns where things are; the first green room of a run always has the Prop Bench), larger green rooms all four. Materials are the currency of all of them: the materials that exist plus what salvaging gives. Nothing here needs a recipe book: every screen shows what goes in, what comes out and what it costs before anything is spent, and every action is one click with a short animation and a sound of its own.

DELIVER
1. The Prop Bench (salvage and repair of a run's clutter): put in any gear: it comes apart into parts by rarity and item level (scrap, fittings, a rare component from rare gear and above, a spark from a unique), shown before the lever is pulled; "Salvage junk" takes everything marked as junk (items-UX) at once with a confirm that lists the yield. The bag's and stash's sell-or-leave problem ends here.
2. The Fitting Room (make a piece better): one piece on the stand: raise its item level towards the depth's level for parts (never above what this depth drops: it lets a favourite piece keep up, it does not outgrow the floor); reroll one affix of the crawler's choice while the others stay (the cost rises per reroll on that piece; the card shows the possible range before the roll); add an empty affix slot to a piece below its rarity's maximum for a rare component. The comparison card of items-UX shows before and after.
3. The Still (brew): three ingredient slots and a burner: combinations of the supplies and ingredients that exist make drinks with clear effects for the next minutes or the next floor (the potions that exist become recipes here; add eight new ones that a build would want: a crit tonic, a block draught, a mana syrup, a Hype fizz...). Recipes are found by trying and are then remembered in a small book on the screen (per player, across runs); a bad mix gives a harmless joke drink, never nothing. Trains Alchemy; the Prop Bench and the Fitting Room train Tinkering.
4. The Grindstone and Polish (prepare for the next fight): a quick buff station with a small skill game (hold while a needle swings; release in the green band): a sharpened edge (more damage for the next two rooms), a polished shield (better block), a tuned focus (cheaper spells); the better the release, the longer it holds; once per piece per green room. A minute of hands-on care that pays in the next fight.
5. Placement and feel: the stations as blocks of the show's backstage (they belong to the green room, not to the place: one set of art) placed by the green room compositions through world.Stations (a hook in world, listed under shared changes), each with a soft idle animation or light, the kit's key hint when looked at, the crawler's hands at work while the screen is open (a clip per station, seen by other players), and a line of the Emcee the first time a crawler uses each (voice script, with a direction). A vendor sells parts dearly and buys them cheaply, so that materials have a price.
