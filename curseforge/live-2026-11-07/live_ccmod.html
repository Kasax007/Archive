Challenge Craft — turn every world into a challenge run

Challenge Craft turns normal survival into replayable challenge runs. Pick from 50 challenges — run one, or stack a brutal combination — and every world you finish earns permanent Lifetime XP that unlocks more challenges and perks. Play the runs you know from YouTubers or invent your own.

New: 🎰 The House Always Wins — a full casino challenge · 🚦 Red Light, Green Light · 🪑 Cushion Only · 🧭 All Biomes · 🔗 Challenge Codes & Presets · 🤖 Bob, an AI rival for Lockout (Early Development Stage)

The modpack is this mod plus shaders, proximity voice chat, a full ambient soundscape, quality-of-life mods and a custom menu — already configured.

Have an idea? Suggest a challenge on Discord and I will build it.

  1. Pick your challenges when creating a world — a single one, or several stacked. Every combination gets a difficulty rating.
  2. Beat the run. Defeat the Ender Dragon to complete the world.
  3. Earn Lifetime XP. The payout scales with your difficulty rating — and it is permanent, carrying across every world and server.
  4. Level up (1–20). Each level unlocks new challenges and permanent perks.
  5. Go infinite. Past Level 20, every 1,000 XP earns an Infinity Star: colored name auras, and one final secret at 20 stars.

Fair play: changing challenges mid-run sets the XP payout to 0, and dying in the Hardcore challenge fails the run.

A complete casino inside your survival world. (Challenge 50 — unlocks at Level 8)

Every item you own is worth Jetons. Cash in at the Croupier's booth at spawn — and keep the bank full, because every 10 minutes the House collects a fee from the whole team. It starts at pocket change and climbs to eight diamonds every ten minutes. Can't pay? The House wins and the run is over.

Farm to pay the House — or gamble to get ahead. Buy the machines from the Croupier and build them anywhere:

  • 🎰 Mine Fever — a 5-reel slot machine with free spins, an expanding lucky item and a 5,000× top prize. Bet a whole stack of items and win it back as a fountain of that item.
  • 🔴 Plinko — drop the ball and pray for the 30× edges
  • 🚀 Crash — the rocket climbs, the multiplier grows. Cash out before it explodes.
  • 🎡 Roulette — a full European table; place your chips straight on the felt

The rules of the House

  • The House always wins — every game pays back 96 % on average. Gambling is the fast way up — and the slow way down.
  • Lost bets bite back — the House sends monsters after you. The bigger the loss, the nastier the wave.
  • Death costs 25 % of your Jetons — then the Croupier deals you a hand of Blackjack. Win and you stand up where you fell, with your whole inventory. Lose and you die for real.
  • No menus at the tables — every game is played right on the machine, with win shows, fanfares and fireworks for the big hits.
  • Co-op ready — the fee is split across the team, and Roulette and Crash rounds are shared.

Jetons are play money that only exists in your world — no real money, no purchases.

Level 1

Lockout Bingo
Teams race on a shared 5×5 board — every goal can only be claimed once. The winning team earns 100 Lifetime XP. No one to race? Play against Bob, the AI rival (Early Development Stage).

Force Item Battle
Every player races to collect their own random target item — a floating icon above your head reveals what's next. Most items when the timer runs out wins 100 Lifetime XP, solo or in teams.

Random Chunk Blocks

500 Blocks = Item

Damage = Item

Level 9

Corrosive Tools

Dice Movement

Challenge Craft is built to be played together. Challenges, Lifetime XP and levels sync to every player, and name tags show each player's level and aura.

  • Lockout Bingo — up to 4 teams race on a shared 5×5 board
  • The House Always Wins — one team, one bank: the House collects its fee from everyone
  • All Biomes — the whole server shares one checklist
  • Symbiotic Bond — the whole server shares one health pool
  • Challenge Codes — send a code, and everyone plays the same setup on the same seed
  • World restart in-game — the server generates a fresh challenge world and archives the old one; no file juggling between runs

Meet Bob — your Lockout rival (Early Development Stage)

Bob is in an early development stage. He learns new skills with every update, but he still makes mistakes — and sometimes dies in very creative ways. Clips and feedback are welcome on Discord.

How to install

  1. Create a new server with BisectHosting.
  2. Select Minecraft 26.3 and Fabric as the server type.
  3. Start the server once so all files generate, then stop it.
  4. Upload Challenge Craft and Fabric API into the mods folder.
  5. Start the server.
  6. Install the same two mods on your client.

⚠️ The mod must be installed on both client and server, and both must run the same Minecraft version.
