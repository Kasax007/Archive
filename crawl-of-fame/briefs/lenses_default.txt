breaks: what breaks play
Try to break what was delivered the way a server full of players would: a crash, a soft lock (a room, a floor or a screen a crawler cannot leave), progress or items lost or doubled, state that does not survive a relog or a server restart or leaks from one run or world into the next (static maps keyed by a player, run or level that are never cleared), a packet from a client that is trusted without checking who sent it and from where, two crawlers doing the same thing at the same moment, a cast of one and a cast of eight, a crawler who dies or leaves in the middle. Read the new code paths for each of these and run the game tests that cover them; write scratch tests under /tmp for the cases that have none and report what fails.

eyes: what a player sees and understands
Look at everything the delivery shows a player, at full size, the way somebody would who downloaded the mod today: the pictures in /home/user/shots/__ID__/ and your own where one is missing (1280x720 at GUI 2, 3 and 4, 854x480 at GUI 2). Report what is unreadable, cut off, overlapping or squeezed; what moves badly (slides, snaps, clips through itself) or does not move at all where it should; textures that are noisy, flat or out of place beside their neighbours; texts that are wrong, unclear, too long for their place or that say something the game does not do; and above all: whether a new player would understand what the thing is and what to do with it without being told. Compare with the standard of docs/UI.md (screens) and of the finished places in /home/user/shots/F6-floor/ and /home/user/shots/F8-floor/ (world and creatures).
