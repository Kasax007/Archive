elsewhere: is this unmistakably another world, and as good as the best we have
The owner complained that everything looked like the show. This place must not. Take the tour sheets in /home/user/shots/__ID__/ and your own pictures (use the place's tour script; 1280x720, GUI 2, default brightness) and put them next to /home/user/shots/F6-floor/ and /home/user/shots/F8-floor/. Judge as somebody who has never seen the mod: within three seconds of leaving the stairwell, is it clear that this is somewhere else (materials, light, sky or ceiling, sound bed, what lives here), or is it a studio set in another colour? Are the two or three characters of the place really different rooms? Does any room look empty, flat, noisy, too dark to read at default brightness, or built from one block? Are props repeated in a way that shows the generator? Then the cast: every kind from the front and the side and in its attack: its own silhouette (not a recoloured kind of another place), a face or a focus the eye finds, movement that reads (walks, tells, hits), textures to the standard of docs/CREATURES.md if that exists; every boss at its stage with its introduction and each act. Then the rule: can a player see what to do before it hurts, in the world and on the card, and is there something to do in most rooms? Every text of the place (card, rule, names, descriptions, the Emcee's lines, the two people): our own, in the show's voice, nothing that names or echoes a book, film, game or show. Report each weakness with its picture.

breaks: the place at every depth and in every season
Try to break the place: generate it at depths 1, 2, 5 and 9 in every size class for twenty seeds each and check connection (every room reachable, stairs up and down, the boss gates, the rule's special rooms present and reachable, light on the main walkways); force it into seasons at each depth (the THEMES1 report says how) and walk in from another place and out again; the rule over a relog, a server restart, a death, a cast of one and of eight, and softened at depths 1 and 2 (the card must say so); every boss: the fight starts, changes acts, ends with the gates open, and resets when the cast leaves or dies; a boss or a rule piece that can be cheesed from outside its room; every new ability once against a blocking, a fleeing and a standing crawler (a hit without a tell is a finding); monsters that leave their room, fall out of the world or get stuck in the place's props; the balance table at depths 1, 5 and 9 against the bands of docs/BALANCE.md (run tools/dev/balance.sh yourself); the server's cost with tools/dev/load.sh 4; the time per chunk of the generator; state kept in statics; packets trusted without checks; the place removed by a data pack while a season holds it. Write scratch game tests under /tmp for what has no test and report what fails.
