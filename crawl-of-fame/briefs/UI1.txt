THE DIRECTION (decided; do not reopen it): the overlay of a modern broadcast. Flat, dark, calm, exact. The show's glamour is an accent, not a frame.
- Colours (ARGB, one class of tokens, nothing hard-coded elsewhere): panel 0xEB14121A, raised surface 0xFF1E1B26, hairline 0xFF2E2A38, text 0xFFF2ECE2, secondary text 0xFFA59DB0, disabled 0xFF6B6476, accent gold 0xFFF2B84B (selection, primary action, progress; nothing else), positive 0xFF6FD08C, negative 0xFFF0645A, info 0xFF6FB7F0. Rarity and school colours stay as they are.
- Shape: plain panels with a one-pixel hairline and corners cut by two pixels; one darker pixel row under a panel as its shadow. No dotted borders, no double lines, no gradients except in bars. The marquee of bulbs survives in exactly two places: the floor card and the title strip of the crawler screen.
- Grid: spacing only from the scale 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 (GUI pixels); panel padding 8, gaps 6, row height 12 or 16. Everything aligns to it; text baselines line up across columns.
- Type: the game's font. Hierarchy by colour, by upper case for small labels (secondary colour), and by a whole-number scale of 2 for the few big titles. Never a fractional text scale. Numbers right-aligned in columns.
- Motion: a screen fades in over 120 ms and rises 4 pixels; tabs cross-fade in 100 ms and the selection marker slides; hover takes 80 ms; bars ease to a new value in 300 ms; numbers count; toasts slide in from the right and stack. Cubic ease-out everywhere, driven by frame time, never by ticks. A client option reduces motion to plain fades (a small client config file, config/crawloffame-client.json: reduceMotion, hudScale 1 or 0.85, damageNumbers; reachable from the crawler screen's new settings corner).
