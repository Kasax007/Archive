DELIVER
1. The NPC entity: one class for all people, looks from the mob module's body plans with own textures (at least 10 different people: the Gaffer, four kinds of vendors, five inhabitants), a name plate, idle behaviour (looks at the nearest crawler, small gestures), never hurt, never pushed away, never leaves its place. The npc module's own director puts them at the named places of world.Spots when a crawler comes near (once per floor instance, saved) and removes them with the floor.
2. Dialogue: a screen with the speaker's portrait, the text typed out, and choices; dialogues are data (data/crawloffame/crawl/dialogue, generated by a script from a compact text format) with conditions (floor, stats, quest state, has item, has coins) and effects (start or finish a quest, open a shop, give or take items and coins, give Fans, teach a hint). Lines are short and original; the Gaffer is a tired, kind stagehand who has seen forty seasons.
3. The tutorial: in the tutorial booth of floors 1 and 2 the Gaffer explains the show in a few short talks that the crawler can skip: the clock and the set strike, stairs, green rooms and the Unboxing Booth, stats and skills, the map, death. The first talk gives a welcome kit (a Copper crate, a few supplies). Besides that, hints: the first time something relevant happens (first crate, first level, first green room, first coins, stairs still closed, ten minutes left), a small hint toast names the key or the place; hints can be switched off in the config and are remembered per player.
4. Vendors: a shop screen (buy and sell, prices by Charm and the Bartering skill, which trains by trading; the stock is rolled per floor instance from the loot tables: concessions = supplies, wardrobe = gear, props = bombs and gadgets, a fence who buys anything and sells one unique at a high price); a vending machine block for green rooms with a small fixed stock. Coins are the currency (item.Coins); every purchase is checked on the server; coins spent count in show.Stats.
5. Inhabitants and quests (floor 3 first, built so that later floors add theirs): quests as data: kill a number of a faction, clear a named room, find a lost prop that the world's containers of a district then hold, bring an item, reach a place, beat a district boss; rewards: Quest crate (Crates.award), coins, Fans. A quest list in the HUD (at most three lines, under the minimap) and a tab "Quests" in the crawler screen; quests belong to the cast, progress is shared.
