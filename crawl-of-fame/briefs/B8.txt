TASK B8: module "world" (packages net.kasax.crawl.world.*, net.kasax.crawl.client.world, resources src/main/modules/world, scripts tools/content/world): the way in. The Stage Door as a real Overworld structure, the flyer that leads to it, vanilla advancements, and named places in rooms for the other modules. Reports to read: A1-world, A2-run (how a curtain outside the dungeon starts or joins a run: the door is its curtain's position, join radius 8 blocks; run.StageDoor is the operator's simple builder), B5-dressing (the set kit and painters you build with), A5-show (Emcee).

WHY: until now a Stage Door only exists through an operator command. A player in a modpack must be able to stumble over one, recognise it at once as something special, and walk in.

DELIVER
1. The Stage Door structure crawloffame:stage_door, generated in the Overworld: a small theatre front built from the set kit and fitting vanilla blocks: a marquee canopy with chasing bulbs and the letters CRAWL OF FAME, poster cases, a red carpet with brass stanchions, a ticket booth, open double doors, and inside a staircase that leads about eight blocks down to a landing with the red curtain across it. It must read from far away (lit at night) and look right from every side (a back wall and a roof, not a facade with a hole behind it). At least three variants (for example the proud original, a run-down one with boarded windows and vines, a small side entrance), chosen by position; palettes adapted to hot, cold and temperate biomes where that is cheap. It stands on the ground (terrain adapted, nothing floating, nothing buried, not in water, not on tree tops). Use the structure mechanism that is robust in 26.3 (templates with a jigsaw start, or a code-built piece); /home/user/ref/raft has working structures of both kinds. Placement as data (structure set: spacing 28, separation 12; biome tag crawloffame:has_stage_door = Overworld land without oceans, rivers and caves) so that pack makers can change it with a data pack; say how in docs/CONFIG.md. /locate structure crawloffame:stage_door must find it.
2. The curtain of a generated door must not be lost: nobody in survival can break or move the curtain blocks or dig them out so that they vanish (curtain blocks are unbreakable outside creative mode, immune to explosions and pistons). Builders in creative mode can still build their own doors with the curtain block, and the operator command keeps working (make it build variant 1 of the real structure).
3. Standing in front of a door for the first time, a player gets one line from the Emcee (show.Emcee) that says what this is and that nobody comes back out except as a winner. Walking through the curtain already starts the run (module run); check the whole way in survival mode with a crawltest script: find the door with /locate, walk the carpet, down the stairs, through the curtain, arrive on floor 1.
4. The Casting Call: an item (a flyer, tab SUPPLIES) that points to the nearest Stage Door when used: direction and distance on the action bar and a short trail of sparks towards it; if none is within reach it says so. Recipe: paper, red dye, gold nugget. It also turns up in a few vanilla chests (villages, shipwrecks, dungeons, ruined portals: added with Fabric's loot table events, low odds) so that players who never read a mod page learn that the show exists.
5. Vanilla advancements, a small own tab "Crawl of Fame" with the mod icon as its symbol: find a Stage Door, step through the curtain, reach floor 2, reach floor 3, win a season, and two or three hidden ones for the curious (titles and descriptions in the Emcee's voice). Granted by vanilla triggers where they exist and from code through run.RunEvents elsewhere. Pack makers build quests on these, so the ids are stable and listed in docs/CONFIG.md.
6. Named places in rooms. world.Spots: a painter marks places while it paints (ctx.spot(name, x, y, z, facing)); Spots.of(slot, floor, room) returns them at run time by running the room's painter against a recording canvas (cached per plan; painters are deterministic). Mark: "gaffer" (tutorial booth), "vendor" (every shop, up to three), "vending" (a wall place in SAFE rooms), "inhabitant" (NPC rooms), "shrine", "objective", "casting.role" and "casting.makeover" (casting office), "boss" (where a boss appears), "treasure". world.Stations: other modules register a block for a name (Stations.register(name, facing -> state)); while painting, a registered station is set at its spot, otherwise a fitting prop stands there. Dress the casting office of floor 3 for its two stations (a reception desk under a CASTING marquee, mirrors and a makeover chair under bulbs, a waiting row). mob.Arenas may keep its own boss place; do not change module mob.
7. Tests: game tests that the structure's template places a complete door (curtain present and reachable from the carpet over walkable blocks), that the curtain survives a survival player's digging and an explosion, that the Casting Call finds a door, that Spots returns the gaffer, vendor and casting places on floors 1 to 3 for several seeds and that they are free standing room, that a registered station is placed. Crawltest pictures in survival: each door variant by day and by night from the front and from behind, the stairs down, the curtain, the arrival; the casting office; the advancement tab. Look at them, fix, look again. Copy the final pictures to /home/user/shots/B8/ for the lead.
