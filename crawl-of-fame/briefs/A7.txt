TASK A7: module "magic", part 2 (packages net.kasax.crawl.magic.role and net.kasax.crawl.client.magic, resources src/main/modules/magic, scripts tools/content/magic): roles and makeovers, the Casting Office. DESIGN section 5 ("Role and makeover") is the specification. Reports to read: B2-profile (stats, signature skills: Profiles.setSignature, Rules), A6-magic (the spell framework: a role's active ability is built on it), A5-show (Stats, Achievements: what a crawler did so far), B3-items and B4-crates (gear kinds, Crates.award), B8-stagedoor if it exists by then (world.Stations: named places in rooms where other modules put their blocks).

DELIVER
1. Roles (the classes of the show), at least 12, named after film crew and performers (for example Stunt Double, Pyrotechnician, Prop Master, Method Actor, Illusionist, Lighting Tech, Set Medic, Animal Wrangler, Leading Role, Fight Choreographer, Sharpshooter, Stage Manager; rename freely, all original). Each role: a tagline by the Emcee, stat bonuses, two or three signature skills (cap 25), three passive perks that change how the crawler plays (not only numbers: for example bombs leave fire, a dodge after every kill, potions splash on cast mates, summons last longer and one more may stand), one active ability on a key with a cooldown and no mana cost (built on the spell framework, own look and sound), and a starting kit (items, a tome, a crate). The roles must cover melee, ranged, explosives, each school of magic, support and Charm play.
2. Makeovers (the races of the show: the makeup and prosthetics department rebuilds the crawler), at least 10, original (no creature of any franchise): body size (scale attribute), attribute changes, one or two passives and one drawback each, and a look that other players see: an overlay on the player's skin (own texture layer per makeover) and where it fits simple extra parts (horns, ears, tail, antennae, a glow). Everything is undone when the run ends for the crawler (death, win, leaving): scale, attributes, look.
3. Offers. What the Casting Office offers depends on how the crawler played: stats spent, skills trained, kill styles and other counters of show.Stats, achievements. Every role and makeover has requirements and a weight; the crawler is offered four roles and four makeovers (never fewer than three), fixed for that crawler and run; a few rare ones only for special feats. A crawler who did nothing special still gets sensible offers.
4. The flow. Two stations as blocks with art: the Casting Desk (role) and the Makeover Chair (makeover). Each opens its screen: cards with a portrait, name, tagline, bonuses, perks and the ability, details on hover, and a "Sign the contract" button with a confirmation, because the choice is final. Signing: a line from the Emcee to the cast, a toast, a jingle, the kit. Only from floor 3 on (config), once per run and crawler. Register both blocks with world.Stations under "casting.role" and "casting.makeover" if that class exists (then the Casting Office of floor 3 gets them from the world module); in any case add an operator command to open the screens.
5. Showing it: the crawler screen's header names role and makeover; a tab "Role" lists perks, the ability with its cooldown and the makeover's traits; the cast list of the HUD and the name plate of other crawlers show the role (icon or short name). The ability key is in the key category of ProfileKeys; the ability's cooldown shows next to the spell bar.
6. Saved in Member.extra; survives relog and restart; the offers too.
7. Tests: game tests for the offers (a melee crawler and a caster get different roles; always at least three), signing (bonuses, signature skills, kit, once only, not before the configured floor), each perk kind at least once, an active ability with cooldown, a makeover's scale and attributes and their removal when the run ends, saving and loading. Crawltest pictures: both screens with their cards, the confirmation, the Role tab, three makeovers in third person (front), the HUD with ability cooldown. Look at them, fix, look again.
