TASK __ID__: items a player understands. The owner of the mod played the interim build and wrote: "Weapons are hard to understand, everything, and in the inventory filter." "It's hard to understand what's going on item wise and what to equip." "Better inventory sorting." "In the inventory I must be able to scroll too." "I love having many different things to try and builds to test." Today a tooltip is a list of coloured affix lines, nothing says whether a piece is better than the one worn or what a weapon type does, the stash is five pages of 54 slots behind five small icons without words, sorting has one order, and nothing scrolls. This task makes every item readable at a glance and the stash and bag pleasant to use. It is built on the UI kit (net.kasax.crawl.client.ui, docs/UI.md is binding; read the report UI1 and the lead's decisions at its end, and look at /home/user/shots/UI1/ first). Reports to read: B3-items (gear, affixes, rarities, sets, uniques), B4-crates (stash, trinkets, booth), R4-item-profile, A8-npc (shops), B2-profile (stats and what they do). No number of the balance changes (docs/BALANCE.md): this task explains and arranges, it does not rebalance.

DELIVER
1. Power, one number per piece: a gear score computed on the server from what the piece really gives (damage per second for weapons, armour and health for armour, block for shields, spell power for foci, each affix weighted by what it is worth: put the weights in one data file and explain them in docs/DESIGN.md), comparable inside a slot. The crawler's total Power (sum over what is worn and slotted) stands in the crawler screen and in the HUD's crawler block, next to "floor N recommends X" (from the floor's item level; a quiet warning colour when far below). Game tests: a piece with a higher item level and the same rolls has more Power; an empty slot counts zero; the total follows equipping.
2. The item card (the kit's Tooltip, for every gear item, everywhere a tooltip shows: bag, stash, shop, loot chest, booth result, chat link): line 1 the name in its rarity colour, line 2 what it is in plain words ("One-handed sword - melee", "Shield - off hand", "Wand - spell focus", "Ring - trinket") and its Power; then the one big number of its kind with the two that make it (weapons: damage per second, with damage and attacks per second; armour: armour; shield: blocked share; focus: spell power), then the affixes as stat rows (label left, value right, in the affix colours that exist), set progress, the unique's effect, the flavour line last and quiet. Under it the comparison with the piece worn in that slot (for rings the weaker of the two; for a weapon the held one): every stat that differs as a green or red difference, and one verdict line: "Upgrade: +14 Power", "Sidegrade", "Weaker: -9 Power". While Shift is held the card shows instead what each stat on it does in one sentence ("Crit chance: the share of your hits that deal crit damage") and the roll range of each affix. Every weapon type, shield, focus and trinket type gets one sentence "how it plays" that is true to the code (read it: reach, speed, what its special does, which stat scales it) and stands on the card.
3. Marks on the icon, in every grid that shows the mod's items (bag, stash, shops, loot chests, booth result): a thin border in the rarity colour, a small green arrow in the corner if the piece is an upgrade for its slot by Power, a dot on pieces picked up since the bag was last opened, a small lock on locked pieces. Drawn by the kit (one decorator used by all screens), cheap (cache per stack; no per-frame allocation).
4. Picking up and equipping: picking up gear shows a toast with icon, name in the rarity colour and the verdict ("Upgrade for Chest: +14 Power"), and for five seconds a key hint "[key] equip" (new key binding, default G) swaps it in; the replaced piece goes to the place the new one came from. In bag and stash a right click on a piece of gear equips it the same way (shift-click keeps its vanilla meaning). A button "Equip upgrades" in the Gear tab lists the swaps it would make (slot, old, new, Power) in the kit's Confirm and makes them. Alt + click locks or unlocks a piece: locked pieces are never sold, deposited, dropped by a drop key press or replaced by "Equip upgrades". All of it validated on the server (the piece must be in the crawler's bag or stash; no duplication: game tests swap in every direction, with a full bag, with a cursed or unequippable case if one exists).
5. The Gear tab as a loadout: the crawler's model, every slot around it (weapon, off hand, four armour pieces, the four trinket slots) with the piece, its Power and its rarity; clicking a slot lists what the bag and the stash hold for it, best first, with the comparison card; one click equips. Active set bonuses and unique effects in words beside it, and a short "build summary": the three highest stats and what they favour ("Agility 14: fast attacks, crits"), so that a player sees what their build is.
6. The stash, rebuilt as one scrolling store (the owner: "I must be able to scroll"): no pages. One grid of nine columns that scrolls (wheel, dragging the scrollbar, Page Up/Down, and by itself while an item is dragged near the top or bottom edge), six rows visible, as many rows as needed up to a cap (config stashSlots, default 270). Above it filter chips with icon AND word (All, Weapons, Armour, Trinkets, Supplies, Explosives, Materials, Other; the count of each beside the word), a search field (matches name, type, rarity, set and affix words: "fire", "crit", "ring"), a toggle "Upgrades", and a sort menu (Type, Power, Rarity, Item level, Newest; remembered per player). Filtering and sorting never move an item to another place in the saved store and never lose one: the server owns the store and the view; write the menu so that taking, placing, shift-clicking, number keys and dragging work under every filter, sort and scroll position, and prove it with a game test that makes some hundred random moves under random views and compares the total contents before and after. Existing saved stashes (five tabs of 54) load into the new store. Buttons with words on hover and key hints: "Deposit loot" (everything in the bag except the hotbar, locked and equipped pieces), "Deposit materials", "Sort bag". The bag below the stash scrolls nowhere (it is the player's 36 slots) but has the same marks.
7. The player's own inventory screen (E) while in a run: the same marks on the slots, the item cards, a small kit panel beside the vanilla window with total Power, "Sort bag" (also a key binding, default unbound... choose a free default and list it in docs), "Deposit loot" when a stash is in reach (it always is: the stash is carried), and the four trinket slots, so that nobody has to find the Gear tab to wear a ring. Must not break with other mods' inventory buttons: keep clear of the recipe book button and test at GUI scale 2, 3 and 4.
8. Selling and junk: shops get "Sell" for single pieces as today plus a junk mark (a key on a hovered piece toggles it; junk is drawn dimmed with a small coin) and "Sell junk" with a confirm that lists the total. A rule in the client config (off by default) marks as junk every piece below a chosen rarity that is not an upgrade.
9. Every list and grid of the mod scrolls: go through every screen (grep for the kit's ListView and for hand-made lists) and make sure that anything that can hold more than fits scrolls by wheel and by dragging its scrollbar, shows that there is more (the kit's fading scrollbar and a soft fade at the cut edge), and keeps its place when its content changes. List in the report what you found.

The Armoury (a page in the crawler screen, reachable from the Gear tab): every weapon type, shield, focus, armour set and trinket type the mod has, each with its sentence, its numbers at the crawler's level and, for sets, the bonuses; pieces the crawler has not found yet are shown as silhouettes with a hint where they drop. This is where a player plans a build.
