2. Interviews. A crawler whose Fans are high enough when they reach a stairwell gets an invitation; accepting it plays between the floors (before the next floor's card): a short talk with the show's late-night host (an invented person with a name and a portrait): three questions drawn from at least thirty, each with three answers in different tones (modest, brash, funny), written fresh. The cast can watch: the others see the questions and answers as Emcee lines. Never blocks the airtime unfairly: the clock of the new floor starts after the interview, the old floor's clock is no longer the crawler's problem.

The Hall of Fame board: a block that everybody can craft and place in the Overworld (a marquee frame); using it opens the Crawl of Fame; while players are near it shows the top three names above it.
