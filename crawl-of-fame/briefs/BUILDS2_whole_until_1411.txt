TASK __ID__: builds, part 2: choices that add up during a run. The owner of the mod wrote: "I love having many different things to try and builds to test, also throughout the runs." Part 1 gave every weapon a character, states and combinations, the rehearsal dummy and two loadouts. This part makes a run grow in a direction the player chooses, differently every time: drafted perks, gear that changes the rules, and the freedom to change one's mind. Reports to read: the report of part 1 and docs/BALANCE.md; A7-roles (roles, abilities, the 46 perk kinds and their hooks in magic.role.Perks: the same mechanism carries this task's perks; do not write a second one), B2-profile (stats, skills, level-ups), B3-items (affixes, uniques, sets), A6-magic (spells), B9-rules (shrines), A9-sponsors, SIDEKICK, the items-UX report (item card, Power, Armoury), UI1 (kit; docs/UI.md is binding). Work in a new module "build" (net.kasax.crawl.build, client.build, resources src/main/modules/build, scripts tools/content/build) and extend others by hooks only.

DELIVER
1. Rewrites (the draft): at fixed moments of a run the writers hand the crawler a rewrite: three cards, pick one. Moments: every boss of a district or zone that the crawler helped kill, every third level, and once at each floor's first green room: about twelve to sixteen picks in a full run. A pick is permanent for the run and saved with the crawler. The screen (kit): three cards with icon, name, one clear sentence, the tags as chips, and a line that says what it does with what the crawler already has ("You hold two Fire rewrites", "Your dagger crits from behind: this doubles that bonus"); one reroll per offer for coins, rising in price; an offer that is not answered waits (a small pulsing chip in the HUD, key to open), never forces a choice in a fight, and survives relog and restart.
2. The pool: at least ninety rewrites, written as data (id, tags, rarity, levels, the hook and its numbers, what it needs and what it excludes) in four rarities; most can be taken again to raise them to level three. Tags: Melee, Ranged, Thrown, Magic, Block, Crit, Fire, Frost, Shock, Poison, Bleed, Summons, Sidekick, Hype, Coins, Set (set pieces), Mobility, Survival, Technique. For every tag at least six rewrites, and for every pair of tags that could meet at least one that joins them (Fire + Block: blocked hits scorch the attacker; Hype + Crit: crit chance rises with Hype; Coins + Survival: lose coins instead of the last tenth of health, once per floor). A handful are bargains with a cost (more damage, no healing from food; faster, but the clock runs faster). No rewrite is a plain "+5 % damage": each changes what the player does or when. Offers are weighted towards what the crawler already uses (weapon type, schools, role, earlier picks) without ever showing three cards of one tag, and exclude what cannot work for them. Roles' own perks stay as they are; say how the two layers stack and cap (docs/BALANCE.md).
3. Gear that changes the rules: twenty-four new unique items with one rule-bending effect each, spread over all slots and floors (the models task's standard for their looks if merged, otherwise icons to the standard of the items scripts and a note): thrown knives return to the hand; a shield that stores blocked damage and releases it with the next technique; boots that leave a burning trail while sprinting; a ring that turns overhealing into a shield; a charm that makes the sidekick's ability cast the crawler's bound spell; a helmet that shows monster health and wind-ups through walls nearby; a staff whose beam chains while the crawler stands still; gloves that turn every fifth hit into a crit; a badge that pays Hype for kills made at low health. And twelve new affixes of the same spirit for rare gear and above (conditional and build-shaping rather than flat), shown on the item card in words.
4. Sets with a third step: every armour set gets a six-piece idea reachable with four armour pieces and two trinkets of the set (add set trinkets), a strong, build-defining bonus written to match what the set already favours.
5. Changing one's mind: a station in green rooms from floor 2, the Script Doctor's desk (a person of our own, a few lines; register for the voice task): refund and re-spend stat points (the first time per floor free, then coins), swap one rewrite for a fresh offer of the same rarity (a rising price), and store or recall one saved build (stats, bar, loadouts) so that two can be compared at the rehearsal dummy. Never during a fight; all validated on the server.
6. Seeing a build: a tab "Build" in the crawler screen (kit): the picks so far in order, grouped by tag with counts, the active set steps and unique effects in words, the three numbers the dummy measured last (damage per second, biggest hit, time to kill the armoured dummy), and a one-line name for the build made from its two strongest tags ("Shock Blocker", "Hype Crit"), which the Emcee uses once in a while and the recap and the Hall of Fame board show. The end-of-run recap lists the build.
