DELIVER
1. States a hit can leave, as ONE small system on the server (one component on the monster, synced compactly; the simple states part 1 built, the elements on affixes, the spells' effects and the room's oil and water all become this system: no second one stays behind): burning, chilled (then frozen), shocked, poisoned, bleeding, wet, oiled, staggered, armour broken, marked. Each has a duration, a stack rule and a cap written down in one table (docs/DESIGN.md), an icon of the kit drawn to the standard of docs/ITEMS-ART.md, and shows as a small row of icons over the monster (above its name plate, never wider than the plate, at most five with "+n") and on the boss bar; a look on the body where it is cheap (a tint, drips, frost, sparks: say which).
2. Five combinations that reward setting up, each with a burst a player cannot miss (a flash, a sound of its own, a word over the monster in the combat-number style): wet + shock chains to neighbours; oiled + fire bursts in a circle; frozen + a heavy blow shatters for bonus damage; bleeding + poison rots (healing reduced, damage over time doubled); marked + any crit pays Hype and extra damage. Bosses resist per kind (data: which states, how much shorter), never immune to everything, and no state skips an introduction or an act change. The Emcee notices a combination the first time and a chain of three (lines into the voice script with a direction). Three achievements.
3. Saved with the crawler, safe over relog, death (the Remains hold both sets) and the trunk.
