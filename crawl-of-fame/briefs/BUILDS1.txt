TASK __ID__: builds, part 1: weapons with a character of their own. The owner of the mod wrote: "I love having many different things to try and builds to test, also throughout the runs" and "weapons are hard to understand". Today the ten melee bases differ in three numbers (damage, speed, reach), bows are the game's bows, and nothing a weapon does can be called a move. After this task every weapon type plays differently, there are twice as many types, hits can set up other hits, and a crawler can measure what a build does. Reports to read: B3-items (bases, affixes, elements, uniques), B2-profile (stats, skills, Combat: how weapon skill and stats enter damage), A6-magic (spells, schools, mana, the spell bar and its key handling), A7-roles (abilities on key G, the 46 perk hooks: reuse the hook mechanism, do not build a second one), A3/A4 (monster statuses and what monsters resist), the reports of the items-UX task (the item card: every new thing must show there) and of the models task (how a weapon gets a model and a texture: every new type needs both, to the same standard), docs/BALANCE.md, UI1 (kit).

DELIVER
1. A technique for every weapon type: one active move on a new key ("Technique", default R; shown beside the spell bar with icon, cooldown sweep and key hint), with a wind-up a player can feel and a clear effect, and one passive trait. It must fit the type and be true to its numbers. Suggestions (improve freely, keep the spread): shortsword: lunge forward, a guaranteed crit on a staggered target / balanced: no penalty anywhere; cleaver: a chop that opens a bleed / more damage to bleeding targets; mace: an overhead blow that staggers and breaks armour for a while / ignores part of armour; dagger: a dash through the target to its back / crits from behind; hatchet: thrown, sticks, comes back when recalled / faster after a kill; prop sabre: a parry stance for half a second, a riposte if hit in it / more damage after a block or dodge; greatsword: a wide sweep through everything in front / hits two more targets at reduced damage; maul: a ground slam in a circle that staggers / knocks down small monsters; halberd: a hooking pull that drags a monster in and trips it / first hit on an approaching target is stronger; boom pole: a vault over a monster or a gap, landing blow / keeps monsters at reach; shortbow: a volley of three / faster draw while moving; crossbow: a bolt that goes through a line / first shot after reload crits; throwing knife: a fan of five / knives can be picked up again; wand: an instant cast of the bound spell at half cost / spells cast faster; staff: a channelled beam of the bound school / more mana; buckler: a bash that interrupts a wind-up (the creature task's wind-ups if merged, the attack timer otherwise) / a perfect block within a few ticks reflects; riot shield: a braced advance that pushes a lane clear / blocks projectiles for mates behind. Techniques scale with the weapon's Power and a stat that suits it, train the weapon's skill, cost nothing but the cooldown (melee) or mana (foci), and are validated on the server.
2. Ten new weapon types, each a real alternative with base, model, texture, icon, sounds, technique, trait, a place in the loot tables from the floor that suits it, and at least one unique: suggestions: spear (thrust line, reach), twin blades (two hands, alternating, a combo counter that speeds up), flail (each hit in a row harder, wild), whip (long reach, pulls, disarms a monster's ability for a moment), megaphone (a cone of sound that pushes and frightens: scales with Charm, a weapon for the charming), spotlight cannon (a beam that eats mana and blinds), trick cards (thrown, bounce between monsters: Agility), confetti launcher (lobbed area burst), stage hook (a grapple: pulls the crawler to an anchor or a monster to the crawler), chain light (a lamp on a cable: swung in an arc, sets alight). Every one built from the studio's world, none from another game.
3. States a hit can leave, shown as small icons over the monster and on the boss bar (kit): burning, chilled (then frozen), shocked, poisoned, bleeding, wet, oiled, staggered, armour broken, marked. Read what exists (elements on affixes, spell effects, the set pieces of "things to do" if merged: oil, water) and unify it into one small system on the server (one component on the monster, synced compactly). Five combinations that reward setting up: wet + shock chains to neighbours; oiled + fire bursts; frozen + a heavy blow shatters for bonus damage; bleeding + poison rots (healing reduced, damage over time doubled); marked + any crit pays Hype and extra damage. Bosses resist per kind (data), never immune to everything; the Emcee notices a combination the first time.
4. The rehearsal dummy: in every green room a dummy that takes hits without dying and shows, on a small board beside it and in a kit panel while fighting it, damage per second over the last ten seconds, the biggest hit, and a breakdown (weapon, crit, each element, states, perks, sidekick). Modes by sneaking + use: one target, three in a row, armoured, moving. This is where a crawler compares two weapons before deciding.
5. Two loadouts: a crawler holds weapon sets A and B (main hand and off hand each) and swaps with a key (default X) with a half-second cooldown; the Gear tab (items-UX task) shows both. Techniques have their own cooldown per set.
6. Monsters answer: a few kinds per floor get a trait that asks for a certain tool (data, shown on the monster's name plate as a small icon and explained in the bestiary line if one exists): armoured (blunt and armour break work best), nimble (area and pulls), shielded front (get behind: dagger, vault), swarm (sweeps, cones), flying (ranged, whip). Sparingly: one in five packs, so that a build has a hard match-up and a good one, not a wall.
7. Balance, measured: extend the balance simulation (BalanceSim, tools/dev/balance.sh) with five build presets that use techniques (fast crit, heavy, reach, ranged, caster) and report clear time and damage taken per preset on floors 1, 4 and 8; tune techniques and new types until no preset is more than a quarter faster or slower than the middle at equal Power, and the plain numbers of docs/BALANCE.md (rooms, bosses) stay in their bands. Put the table into docs/BALANCE.md.
8. Tests and proof: game tests for every technique (effect, cooldown, server validation, skill training), every new type (drops, equips, technique), every state and combination, the dummy's numbers against a known hit sequence, loadout swapping (no duplication, relog), the monster traits. Crawltest pictures: every weapon type held and mid-technique (first and third person), the state icons, each combination's burst, the dummy board and panel, the technique slot in the HUD at GUI 2 and 3, the item card of a new type; sheets to /home/user/shots/__ID__/. Look at them: can the types be told apart in the hand, and does each move read?
9. Docs: docs/DESIGN.md (a table: type, trait, technique, stat), docs/DATAPACKS.md (weapon types, techniques, states, monster traits as data where they are), docs/CONFIG.md (keys), README.md.

Keep what works: existing items, affixes, saved gear, roles and their abilities (key G stays theirs), spells.
