TASK AUDIO: module "audio" (packages net.kasax.crawl.audio and net.kasax.crawl.client.audio, resources src/main/modules/audio, scripts tools/content/audio): the sound of the show. DESIGN section 10. Until now every module plays vanilla sounds through one replaceable place each. Reports to read: A5-show (show.Cue, ShowSounds.install, Crowd), A3-mobs and A4-mobs23 (sounds per faction), B3-items and B4-crates (crate, potion, bomb sounds), A6-magic (sounds per school), B5-dressing and B7-dressing23 (biomes per floor), B6-map (ping), A7-roles, B9-rules (traps) where they exist.

TOOLS ON THIS MACHINE: python3 with numpy (no scipy), ffmpeg with libvorbis. Minecraft needs Ogg Vorbis; mono for positional sounds, 44.1 kHz. Sources on the network: incompetech.com (music by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0, credit required) and opengameart.org (use CC0 only) are reachable; kenney.nl, freesound.org and pixabay are blocked. Every file that is not synthesised by your script needs its source, author and licence in CREDITS.md at the repository root; nothing without a clear licence.

DELIVER
1. A synthesiser script (tools/content/audio/synth.py): small building blocks (oscillators, noise, envelopes, filters by FFT or simple IIR, reverb by a few delays, pitch slides, chords) and one function per sound, writing WAV, then Ogg through ffmpeg. The sound of a game show: bright jingles, a studio organ, brass stabs, rim shots, a sad trombone, buzzers and bells, a drum roll with a cymbal, a timpani, a slide whistle, a ticking clock; applause, cheering and gasps built from many small bursts (clapping is band-limited noise bursts at random times: it can sound right); laughter may come from a CC0 recording if synthesis does not convince. Listen with your eyes: plot or print envelope and spectrum, check loudness (peak and RMS per file, normalised by category) and that nothing clips or ends in a click.
2. Sound events with subtitles (lang keys) for everything the mod does: the Emcee's jingle and banner, each show.Cue (applause, gasp, laughter, fanfare, sting...), achievement, level up, crate won by tier, the unboxing (shake, lid, an item per rarity), coins, potion fizz and sickness, each bomb, stairs open, the time warnings, the countdown ticks, the set strike alarm and the collapse, death, victory, the floor card, the boss title card, a boss's acts, gates, the Stage Door's curtain, map ping, each spell school (cast, fly, hit), traps, the booth, the stash, UI clicks of our screens. Monsters: per faction a voice (idle, hurt, death, attack) made from synthesis or by combining vanilla sound files by reference with other pitches (sounds.json may point to vanilla files; do not copy them).
3. Music with a small system on the client: situations (Stage Door in the Overworld near a door, green room, exploring per floor, fight, boss, the last five minutes, set strike, victory, the recap) with cross-fades, on the music volume slider, never two tracks at once, vanilla music silenced inside the dungeon. Tracks: pick from incompetech (lounge for green rooms, three exploring moods for floors 1 to 3, a fight loop, a boss theme, a tense clock piece, a fanfare), cut to loops of at most two and a half minutes, 96 kbit/s; the jar must stay under 25 MB in total. Ambience per floor through its biome (a low hum, far applause through walls, drips, a ventilation drone).
4. Wiring: install your sounds through the hooks of the modules (ShowSounds.install and the others; add a hook where a module still plays a vanilla sound directly, as a small listed change). No module may play a vanilla placeholder afterwards, except where vanilla is right (footsteps, block sounds, vanilla items).
5. CREDITS.md at the repository root: music and any recording with title, author, licence and link, in the form the licences ask for; the same credits in a lang entry shown by the command /crawl credits.
6. Tests: a script check that every sound event in the code has a sounds.json entry, a subtitle and existing files, and that no file is silent, clipped or longer than its category allows; the client log of a crawltest run through a floor shows no missing sound warnings. A short report of loudness per category. Copy three or four spectrogram or waveform sheets to /home/user/shots/AUDIO/ for the lead.
