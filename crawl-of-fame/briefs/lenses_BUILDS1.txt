balance: ten new weapon types, techniques and states against the numbers the game stands on
Every weapon type now has a technique, ten types are new, states and combinations exist, and a crawler carries two weapon sets. Find what is too strong, too weak or broken: for every base and technique compute damage per second, reach, block and control from the code and from tools/dev/balance.sh at depths 1, 5 and 9 with the same item level and Power, and list them side by side (a type that wins everywhere, or one nobody would pick, is a finding); infinite or near-infinite loops (a technique that resets its own cooldown, a state that reapplies itself, a combination that triggers a combination, weapon swapping that skips cooldowns or animation locks, a cancel that doubles a hit); states on bosses (does a stun, a freeze or a knock-up skip an act, interrupt an introduction, lock a boss for a whole fight, or stack past the stated cap) and on crawlers by monsters; two-handed weapons with an off hand piece (the lead decided: both hands are taken); thrown and ranged techniques through walls and gates; techniques used while blocking, casting, riding, falling, in a screen, during a boss card; charges and ammunition after a relog and a server restart; the rehearsal dummy's numbers against a real monster's; Power and the item card for the new types (does the upgrade arrow tell the truth across types); packets trusted without checks (a technique from a weapon that is not held, faster than its cooldown). Write scratch game tests under /tmp and report what fails, with the numbers.

feel: does each weapon type play differently in the hands
The owner asked for "more weapon types with their own way of playing". Take every type into a fight (a script of your own on the task's rehearsal dummies and against three monsters, pictures at 1280x720 GUI 2 in first and third person): does its basic attack differ in rhythm, reach and arc so that it is felt and seen, not only read; is the technique's wind-up, hit and recovery visible (an animation, an arc, a sound, a mark on the floor) and is its key shown where the player looks; do states show on the monster and in the HUD so that a combination can be planned; is the second weapon set one key press and readable on the HUD at 480x270 GUI pixels; does the item card say in one sentence how the type plays, and is that true; are the ten new types recognisable in the hand without their tooltip (they need the models of the items task: say what is missing). Compare two types that should be opposites (a dagger and a maul, a whip and a shield bash) and say whether a player would feel the difference blindfolded by the timing alone. Report each type that plays like another with a different number.
