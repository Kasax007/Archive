Beach Craft brings the open-sea survival of the game Raft into Minecraft.
Step through a driftwood portal, build your raft and sail an ocean of islands, reefs and drowned ruins, all the way down to the bottom of the sea.

 

Tides of the Deep: a calm 21-minute tour of everything in Beach Craft 3.1, filmed with shaders.

 

 

On the other side, open the castaway's chest on your platform: the Seafarer's Almanac explains everything, and the Expedition Journal always knows where to go next.

 

Out at sea you live on what the current brings: hook the debris, purify water, grill your catch and farm on deck. Bruce the shark bites the edges of your raft, so reinforce them.

 

New in 3.1: build a raft block by block and sail the whole thing like a ship. Chests, furnaces, farms and redstone keep working at sea, and the ready-made Expedition Raft is still there too.

 

Palm, jungle, mangrove and volcanic isles, reefs, kelp forests, frost floes and dark trenches: twelve biomes, with day and night out at sea.

 

Seven story places and twelve kinds of side places, from a lighthouse and a stilt village to a drowned city, rusted rigs and sunken wrecks.

 

New in 3.1: eight people still live out here. Talk to them for hints and rumours; most of them have a welcome gift and trade for pirate gold and pearls.

 

Most creatures are friendly. Jellyfish sting, anglerfish lurk in the dark, and Bruce the shark goes after your raft.

 

Helmet, oxygen tank, wetsuit and flippers take you deep. A harpoon gun, a sea scooter and a helmet lamp come along.

 

84 building blocks and 59 decorations, from weathered docks and pirate ships to research domes and drowned skyscrapers.

 

Logs, letters and research terminals lead you from place to place. Gather four sigils, forge the Tidewarden Key and open the sanctum where the Leviathan waits.

 

Beach Craft is part of the Dreamcraft modpack, where a whole quest chapter guides you across the Endless Sea.

Details: requirements, existing worlds, game rule and music credits

Requirements

  • Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer and Fabric API.
  • Team Reborn Energy is included, so machines from mods like Tech Reborn can connect.

Existing worlds

  • Worlds from 3.0 work: the people move into the places you have already found the first time you come near. The new flowers and the better structure placement show up in newly explored chunks.
  • Worlds from before 3.0: chunks of the Endless Sea you explored keep their old terrain. Sail farther out, or start a new world, to find the islands and story places.
  • The castaway's chest only waits on new arrival platforms. Already have one? Craft an Expedition Journal (book, compass and paper), then sneak and use it at sea for a chart to the first story place.

Game rule

  • /gamerule raft:shark_bites false stops sharks from biting rafts. They still circle rafts and still hunt swimmers.
