TITLE (suggestion)
Beach Craft 2.0 for Minecraft 26.3 - every feature in one review

DESCRIPTION
A review of everything in Beach Craft (the Raft mod) for Minecraft 26.3: the driftwood portal, the raft ocean,
debris, the plastic hook, the item catcher, trash cubes, the water purifier, the grill, crop plots, the scarecrow,
Bruce the shark, the receiver, the paddle, titanium, the metal detector, solar power, the quarry, the furnaces and
all the decoration - recorded with the real game sounds.

Beach Craft is free on CurseForge: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/beach-craft
