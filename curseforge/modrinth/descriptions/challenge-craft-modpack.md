<p><strong>FAQ</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>How do I switch the shader pack?</strong> Options, Video Settings, Shader Packs. Complementary Reimagined is on by default, BSL and Bliss are installed too.</li>
<li><strong>Item textures are gone while shaders are on?</strong> Type <strong>/challengecraft_fix_textures</strong>. It reloads the shader pack.</li>
<li><strong>Can I play alone?</strong> Yes. Create a world, pick your challenges in the Challenges tab and go.</li>
<li><strong>Do the casino chips cost real money?</strong> No. Chips are play money that only exist in your world.</li>
</ul>
