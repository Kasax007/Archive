stories: are these things a player tells a friend about
The bar of the task: no rescue is a lever beside a cage, no gadget is a stat potion, no bounty is a monster with more health. Play three places in survival (a studio department, Hollowmere Manor and one more; 960x540 GUI 2 and a GUI of 480x270) and judge each as a player: does a newcomer see and hear from the door that somebody needs help and what to do about it; is there tension (the pack alive, the countdown) and a moment of relief; is the person somebody (a face, a body, a voice line that fits the fix and the place: read the lines aloud; none may repeat another's joke), do they walk off properly and can they be thanked; is each reward felt. For every gadget: does its item card say what it does in one line, does using it look and sound like using a thing (the clip, frame by frame in a strip), does it open a play that was not there before (try each together with a piece of the room), is any of them useless or always the best. The bounty: does the poster make a player want to go, is the quarry recognisable in a crowd, is the fight different from its kind's. The board and the poster against the kit (docs/UI.md) at every size. Report what is flat, unclear, repetitive or ugly, with the picture.
