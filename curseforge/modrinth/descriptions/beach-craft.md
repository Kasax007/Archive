<h3 align="center"><strong>Made with love, sweat and tears<br>Fabric only</strong></h3>
<p align="center"><a href="https://bisecthosting.com/kasax007"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/bisect.png" alt="BisectHosting - 25% off with code KASAX007"></a></p>
<p align="center"><strong>Beach Craft brings the open-sea survival of the game <em>Raft</em> into Minecraft.<br>Step through a driftwood portal, build your raft and sail an ocean of islands, reefs and drowned ruins, all the way down to the bottom of the sea.</strong></p>
<p align="center">&nbsp;</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NdFPSYZ32R4"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/video.jpg" alt="Watch the 21-minute tour: Tides of the Deep"></a></p>
<p align="center"><em>Tides of the Deep: a calm 21-minute tour of everything in Beach Craft 3.1, filmed with shaders.</em></p>

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/glance_33.png" alt="Beach Craft at a glance: 12 biomes, 19 places, 8 people, 18 creatures, 198 blocks and a boss"></p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_start.png" alt="Getting started"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/flow_start.png" alt="The way into the Endless Sea: driftwood, Ancient Broken Ring, Ancient Energy, portal frames, Energy Staff, light the portal"></p>
<p align="center">On the other side, open the castaway's chest on your platform: the <strong>Seafarer's Almanac</strong> explains everything, and the <strong>Expedition Journal</strong> always knows where to go next.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_survive.png" alt="Survive"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/blocks_classic.png" alt="Raft survival blocks and items"></p>
<p align="center">Out at sea you live on what the current brings: hook the debris, purify water, grill your catch and farm on deck. Bruce the shark bites the edges of your raft, so reinforce them.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_shipwright.png" alt="Build your own raft"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/flow_raft.png" alt="Your own raft: foundations, helm, power, moor"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/raft_31.jpg" alt="A raft built from blocks, sailing"></p>
<p align="center">Build a raft block by block and sail the whole thing like a ship. Chests, furnaces, farms and redstone keep working at sea, and the ready-made Expedition Raft is still there too.</p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.2:</strong> stack a mast and join sails into one big sheet, drop the anchor with a winch, fuel the motor through its own screen and watch the wheel turn as you steer. Sneak and use the wheel to moor.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_world.png" alt="A world of islands"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/world_31.jpg" alt="Biomes of the Endless Sea"></p>
<p align="center">Palm, jungle, mangrove and volcanic isles, reefs, kelp forests, frost floes and dark trenches: twelve biomes, with day and night out at sea.</p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.2:</strong> more open sea between the islands, palm beaches with lagoons, turtles, parrots, rabbits and foxes on the islands, and flocks of seagulls in the sky.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_places.png" alt="Places to explore"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/places_31.jpg" alt="Places of the Endless Sea"></p>
<p align="center">Seven story places and twelve kinds of side places, from a lighthouse and a stilt village to a drowned city, rusted rigs and sunken wrecks.</p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.2:</strong> most places are guarded now, and the guards get tougher the further the story leads you. Their chests hold more, too.</p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.4:</strong> the big places are rarer and lie further apart, they stand on the ground, and many of their barrels and crates hold loot now. With Lootr installed every player gets their own loot.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_people.png" alt="People of the Sea"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/people_31.jpg" alt="The eight people of the sea"></p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.1:</strong> eight people still live out here. Talk to them for hints and rumours; most of them have a welcome gift and trade for pirate gold and pearls.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_creatures.png" alt="Creatures"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/creatures_31.jpg" alt="Creatures of the Endless Sea"></p>
<p align="center">Most creatures are friendly. Jellyfish sting, anglerfish lurk in the dark, and Bruce the shark goes after your raft.</p>
<p align="center"><strong>New in 3.2:</strong> scavengers, reef guardians and corsair crews guard the places, led by the Scavenger Chief, the Corsair Captain and the Reef Warden.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_diving.png" alt="Diving"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/diving_31.jpg" alt="Diving gear"></p>
<p align="center">Helmet, oxygen tank, wetsuit and flippers take you deep. A harpoon gun, a sea scooter and a helmet lamp come along.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_decor.png" alt="Build and decorate"></p>
<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/blocks_3.png" alt="Building blocks and decorations"></p>
<p align="center">84 building blocks and 59 decorations, from weathered docks and pirate ships to research domes and drowned skyscrapers.</p><p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_cozy.png" alt="Cozy living"></p><p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/cozy_33.jpg" alt="Cozy furniture, lights and decor"></p><p align="center"><strong>New in 3.3:</strong> benches, loungers, armchairs, tables and serving plates, shelves and baskets, lanterns you can switch on and off, a steel drum, parasols and lots of beach decor. Sit down somewhere cozy and you get <strong>Rested</strong>: the more kinds of cozy things around you, the longer it lasts.</p><p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_kitchen.png" alt="The island kitchen"></p><p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/kitchen_33.png" alt="Island crops, dishes, drinks and treats"></p><p align="center"><strong>New in 3.3:</strong> sweet potatoes, chili peppers, bananas, rice and sea grapes that grow under water. Cook curry and chowder in the cooking pot, roll sushi, blend a smoothie, and let a fish trap do the fishing.</p><p align="center"><strong>New in 3.4:</strong> watch the cooking pot cook: the ingredients stir in the pot, the water takes the colour of the dish and a chime rings when it is ready. It only takes what fits a dish and tells you what is still missing.</p><p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_gear.png" alt="Gear and gadgets"></p><p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/gear_33.png" alt="Weapons, armour and tools"></p><p align="center"><strong>New in 3.3:</strong> a boomerang that brings back floating loot, a machete, a shark tooth spear, shell armour and an explorer's outfit with set bonuses, a compass that finds wrecks, a captain's spyglass and fishing bait.</p><p align="center"><strong>New in 3.4:</strong> the Driftwood Fishing Rod with its own tackle box: load Fish Bait and Golden Lures, and every cast uses one of each.</p>
<p><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kasax007/Kasax_Raft_Mod/26.3/docs/curseforge/img/strip_story.png" alt="Tides of the Deep"></p>

<p align="center">Logs, letters and research terminals lead you from place to place. Gather four sigils, forge the Tidewarden Key and open the sanctum where the Leviathan waits. Since 3.2 it keeps to the deep of its pool and fights harder.</p>

<summary><strong>Details: requirements, existing worlds, game rule and music credits</strong></summary>

<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer and Fabric API.</li>
<li>Minecraft 1.21.1: Beach Craft 3.4 with everything from 26.3 (1.21.1 has no shelves and no spears, so there the shelves are Beach Craft's own block and the Shark Tooth Spear is a weapon of its own; optional JEI 19.x and Lootr 1.11, which needs Cloth Config), Fabric Loader 0.16 or newer and Fabric API. Minecraft asks you to confirm "experimental settings" when you create a world, as it does for every mod with its own dimension.</li>
<li>Team Reborn Energy is included, so machines from mods like Tech Reborn can connect.</li>
<li>Optional: Just Enough Items shows the cooking pot, fish trap and crop recipes; Lootr gives every player their own loot from chests, barrels and crates.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Existing worlds</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Worlds from 3.2 work as they are. Wild island crops and sea grapes grow in newly explored chunks; seeds also come with flotsam and in chests.</li>
<li>Worlds from 3.1 work: the new sea of 3.2 (more water, fewer structures, more animals) shows up in chunks you have not explored yet, so where old and new parts meet an island can end in a straight edge.</li><li>Worlds from 3.0 work: the people move into the places you have already found the first time you come near. The new flowers and the better structure placement show up in newly explored chunks.</li>
<li>Worlds from before 3.0: chunks of the Endless Sea you explored keep their old terrain. Sail farther out, or start a new world, to find the islands and story places.</li>
<li>The castaway's chest only waits on new arrival platforms. Already have one? Craft an Expedition Journal (book, compass and paper), then sneak and use it at sea for a chart to the first story place.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Game rule</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>/gamerule raft:shark_bites false</strong> stops sharks from biting rafts. They still circle rafts and still hunt swimmers.</li>
</ul>
