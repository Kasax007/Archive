TASK __ID__: places drawn at random. The owner of the mod played floors 1 and 2 and wrote: "Everything I saw on floor 1 and 2 is show themed. There should be very many different themes that are chosen at random. Each with its own mobs, style, dungeon style, rooms and so on." Today a floor's number decides everything: floor 4 is always the Boiler Room with its cast, its rule and its people. After this task the number is only the depth, and what a floor IS (its place) is drawn per run from a pool, so that no two seasons run through the same places in the same order; the pool then grows by many new places (other tasks, each adding one place without touching your code). This task is the architecture and nothing else: no new content, no change to how any existing place plays when it stands at its old depth. It touches every module, so read first and change little in each: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and docs/DESIGN.md, docs/FLOORS.md, docs/BALANCE.md, the reports A1-world, A2-run, A3/A4 (mobs, rosters), B9-rules, A8-npc, B3-items, A5-show, B6-map, AUDIO-sound, SIDEKICK and the floor reports F4 to F10 (how each floor hooks in).

THE MODEL (decided; do not reopen it)
- A PLACE is everything that makes a floor what it is: an id (the theme ids that exist: understage, prop_storage, backlot, boiler_room, sunken_archive, overgrowth, cold_storage, high_roller_hall, dead_air, final_curtain), its name, look and room painters, its layout wishes (special rooms, objectives, how many districts it likes), its roster of monsters and bosses, its rule, its people and quests, its unique items, its ambience and music, its lines, its achievements. A place is registered once (world.Places, a successor of the FloorDefs table) and every module looks its own part up by the place id, never by a number.
- DEPTH is the floor's number in the run, 1 to the last: it decides monster level, item level and shop bands, the size class of the layout (depth 1 small, depth 2 medium, from 3 large: the numbers of today's floors 1, 2 and 3+), airtime, how hard the place's rule bites (a factor the rule reads: 0.6 at depth 1, 0.8 at depth 2, 1 from depth 3; each rule applies it to its rates and damages in one place), and the services of the show that belong to a depth and not to a place: the tutorial rooms at depths 1 and 2, the sidekick's carrier from the configured depth, the Casting Office at depth 3 (today a part of the Backlot and of Floor3Rule: it becomes a room and a stairs condition that any place can host at depth 3, dressed by a small kit that fits every look; the Backlot keeps its town), the finale at the last depth.
- THE SEASON: when a run starts it draws its order: for every depth but the last a place from the pool without repeats, by weight, honouring each place's allowed depths (data: min and max depth, a weight; the Final Curtain is the only place for the last depth and for nothing else). The order is saved with the run, survives relog and restart, is the same for the whole cast, and is sent to the clients (floor card, tab list, map title, the crawler screen: "Floor 4: The Boiler Room" stays the form). A config key seasonOrder: "random" (default), "classic" (today's order) or a list of place ids; a player's very first run starts in the Understage (the gentlest place) and is random from depth 2. Commands for operators and tests: /crawl season (shows the order), /crawl season set <depth> <place>, and /crawl floor <n> keeps working.
- PLACES ARE DATA where they can be: data/crawloffame/crawl/places/<id>.json (name key, look id, roster id, rule id, weight, min and max depth, the layout wishes) so that a data pack can change weights and depths, remove a place from the pool or add one that reuses existing looks and rosters; docs/DATAPACKS.md explains it with an example. Rosters are no longer named by floor number (keep reading old files).
