TASK __ID__: the show goes on air. The owner of the mod wrote: "Can we also have the show automatically generated and being aired on a big TV in the green rooms or kitchens where NPCs can give me food? This would be fun." A crawler who reaches a green room sits down with a plate and watches tonight's episode: their own run, cut into a programme. You build the programme (generated from what really happened), the big screen, the canteen and its cook. Everything our own: our anchor, our sponsors, our jokes (tools/dev/check_names.py). Reports to read: A5-show (occasions, stats, recap, Hall of Fame, the green room monitor that exists: keep what it does or fold it into this, and say which), A9-sponsors (sponsors, interviews, fan letters), A2-run (members, floors), B5/B7 and the floor reports (green room compositions: RoomPainters.lounge and the per-floor variants), A8-npc (people, dialogue, shops), the voice task's report and docs/VOICE.md (speakers, script, the contract with the lead), UI1 (kit; docs/UI.md is binding), part 1 of "things to do" (searching, the craft-service trolley, ingredients) if merged. A new module "broadcast" (net.kasax.crawl.broadcast, client.broadcast, resources src/main/modules/broadcast, scripts tools/content/broadcast).

DELIVER
1. Moments (server): a bounded log per run of what is worth showing, filled from events that exist (grep the show module's stats and occasions first; add hooks only where nothing fires): boss and elite kills with their time, the biggest single hit, multi-kills, narrow escapes (health under a tenth and alive ten seconds later), deaths with their cause, set kills and bonus rounds if those modules exist, crates opened and the best piece, level-ups, a role signed, a sidekick taken or carried off, Hype peaks, floors reached with the time. Each moment: who, what, where (floor, room kind), numbers, a weight. Saved with the run.
2. The programme (server builds it, client plays it): whenever a crawler enters a green room, a running order of eight to twelve segments is cut from the moments since their last visit plus standing features, sent to the clients in the room, and looped with fresh picks until they leave. Segment kinds (each a few seconds to twenty, each with its own layout, ident sting and anchor line):
   - Cold open and titles (the show's logo, tonight's cast with their faces from their skins, floor and episode number = floor).
   - Highlights: the top moments as action cards: the crawler's face or model, the monster's portrait, a caption written from the data ("<name> ends the Rat King in 41 seconds"), the number big.
   - Slow-motion replay: for the single best moment a staged tableau: the crawler's model and the monster's model posed against the room kind's backdrop, a slow push-in, the damage number landing.
   - The standings: the cast ranked by Fans, kills, damage and deaths, bars easing in; a second board with the server's Hall of Fame.
   - The ratings: Hype over the last floor as a line that the anchor comments on (rising, flat, falling).
   - Coming up: the next floor: its name, its rule in one sentence, a boss in silhouette with one true hint, the hazards as icons: useful to someone who has never been there.
   - Stage report, live: stairs open or not, bosses alive, the clock, where the cast mates are on a small map.
   - In memoriam: crawlers lost tonight, with the cause, in black and white.
   - A word from our sponsors: a spot for one of the mod's sponsors (animated product card, jingle, the ads announcer's read); the crawler's own sponsor's spot runs more often.
   - Viewer mail and polls: a fan letter read out, or a poll made from the stats ("six in ten viewers expect <name> to buy a helmet").
   - Interview cut: a question and the answer the crawler gave in their last interview, if any.
   - Filler for a quiet night (no moments): the anchor stalls, a test card, a rerun joke: it must still be worth a look.
   The text of captions is assembled from data with the names in it; the anchor's spoken lines never contain names (write them for the voice task's script: speaker "anchor", at least 140 lines across the segment kinds and situations, and "announcer_ads" with one read per sponsor); captions and voice together tell it.
3. The big screen: a wall of screen blocks that forms one picture of any size the room offers (4x3 by default), with a frame, a glow on the room, and a picture that is readable from eight blocks and crisp from two. Draw the programme onto it in the world (decide how after reading how 26.3 renders: an off-screen target drawn as a quad, or direct drawing on the plane; say why; it must cost nothing when no screen is in view and little when one is: measure frames with one and with four screens in view). The same programme opens full-screen through the kit when a crawler uses the screen or sits down (richer: models turn, numbers count), with a row of buttons: skip segment, mute, standings, coming up. Sound comes from the screen (positional, the "tv" preset), is quiet by default, and is silent for crawlers outside the room.
4. Seats: sofas and canteen chairs a crawler can sit on (sneak to stand up); sitting in front of the screen for ten seconds gives "Feet up" (a mild regeneration while in the room and a small bonus for the next floor's first minutes). Sidekicks lie down beside their crawler if that module exists.
5. The canteen: every green room gets a kitchenette (counter, urn, fridge, a hatch with a bell; skinned per floor's theme by the looks: a hook in world, listed under shared changes), and from floor 2 the larger green rooms become a canteen with the cook, a person of our own with a name, a look, a temper and about forty lines (register for the voice task). The cook hands every crawler one plate per green room visit, chosen from three dishes of the day (each a clear bonus for the next ten minutes or the next floor: healing over time, speed, coin find, resistance to the floor's rule), remembers what the crawler took last time, and cooks specials from ingredients a crawler brings (found by searching; a short recipe list as data, discovered by trying: a recipe card in the kit's style). Eating is an animation with a plate in the hand, not a click. A fridge gives one snack per floor, the urn a coffee (short haste, the cook's remark). The sidekick gets a bowl.
6. Multiplayer: everyone in the room sees the same programme at the same position (the server's clock), sits where they like; a cast's moments are everyone's; names of cast mates appear only for members of that cast.
7. Tests: game tests for the moments log (each kind once, bounded, saved), the running order (non-empty on a quiet night, picks the heaviest moments, no segment twice in a row), the cook (one plate per visit, three dishes fixed per visit, a recipe, the bonus applied and ended), seats, the sync to a second player. Crawltest pictures: every segment kind on the wall screen from two and from eight blocks and full-screen at GUI 2 and 3, a green room with the screen and kitchenette on four different floors' skins, the canteen with the cook, a crawler sitting and eating, the recipe card; sheets to /home/user/shots/__ID__/. Look at each segment as a viewer: does it look like television, and would you stay for the next one?
