## Text (GitHub-Issue, Englisch)

> Hi anvian! Create And Space is such a great idea – Create with Steam 'n' Rails, Deco and Copycats+ for building, and then Ad Astra to take your factory all the way into space. Your page asks for mod suggestions here, so I'd like to suggest one.
>
> I make Quest Icons, an FTB-Quests-style quest book that runs on Fabric 1.20.1: chapters, quest maps, tasks, rewards, loot crates and teams, with an in-game editor (forms and pickers, no SNBT) and 470 icons. A quest book could lead new players from their first water wheel to their first rocket launch. If you'd like to try it, I'd love to write you a free starter chapter for the pack and help set it up – just tell me what you need!
>
> https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/quest-icons
>
> Thanks for maintaining Create And Space for so long! Kasax (Discord: discord.gg/zuZVtVvJaY)
