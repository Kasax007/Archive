echoes: does the game still read as one particular work's skeleton
Read docs/DESIGN.md and the game's texts as someone who knows the well-known works of the genre (a deadly dungeon crawl as a television show; LitRPG with classes, races, loot boxes, an audience and a host) and play the first three floors and a boss: after this task, is there still a rule or a sequence of rules that a reader of one particular work would recognise as that work's (a class and race chosen at a fixed moment, boxes that only open in one kind of room, a ladder of bosses by area, a talk show at fixed points, names of ranks or boxes)? For each, show it on screen with a picture or the text, name the work and the rule, and propose the smallest change of our own. Also check that every text, card, line and test that described the old rules now describes the new ones: grep for "floor 3", "depth 3", "green room" with crates, "district boss", "zone boss", "floor boss" in what players see and hear.

breaks: the new moments in a real game
Play the changed rules in survival as a player would, with sound (CT_AUDIO=1 and tools/crawltest/listen.py), alone and with two crawlers, and try to break them: the first district boss down on depth 1, on depth 4, by a mate while you are elsewhere, by you after a death and a new run, with the boss killed during a relog; the stairs after it with and without a role; the Casting Office in a green room before and after; a crate opened in a corridor, on stairs, in water, in a boss arena, during a sidekick fight, with a full bag, with the ten seconds cut by a step and by a hit, two crates at once, a relog mid-countdown; the Topiary Beast under the lights, without them, with the lights switching mid-fight, over a reload. Report every case that misbehaves with the picture or the log, a game test that fails on it, and the smallest fix.
