Since then the lead has played the game at the size most players see it: a 1920x1080 monitor at the game's default GUI scale gives 480x270 GUI pixels. THE BAR: at 480x270 a fight is readable at a glance: nothing covers the crosshair or the middle fifth of the screen, no sentence is on screen twice, no text is cut, no two panels lie on each other, and every moment of the show (arriving, a boss, a death, a win) looks like the show and not like the game underneath it.
